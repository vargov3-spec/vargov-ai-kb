## Light in an atrium

**Subheading:** The one room where a composition is seen from above, from the side and from below at once.

An atrium is not an enlarged lobby. In a lobby the piece is read from below, and what
works is the silhouette. In an atrium people look at it from the upper galleries, from the
lift, from the stairs — and what reads there is not the silhouette but the structure: how
the elements stand in relation to one another, where it is sparse and where it is dense.
A composition that is flawless seen from below can turn flat from the third floor.

So the first design decision is not size but **the direction of the gaze**. A composition
for an atrium is built as a vertical order: it fills the volume between the levels rather
than hanging as a patch under the ceiling. Its height is set by the gallery levels, not by
the ceiling.

The second is maintenance. In an atrium there is often no floor beneath the composition —
a walkway, a conservatory, water. A tower cannot be placed there, so the question of how
to take it down and clean it is settled at the design stage or never.

### What to consider

**The lowest point follows the galleries, not the floor.**
Aim for the lowest point at the level of the upper gallery, so the composition reads whole
from every floor. The general rule — no lower than 2.1 m above the floor — is usually
cleared many times over here; the constraint comes from above.

**The order is calculated against the light.**
In an atrium the composition is seen against glazing and against the opposite gallery. The
density of elements is chosen so the piece does not collapse into a silhouette in
backlight: sparse above and denser below, or the reverse — decided for the specific volume.

**Access is resolved before installation.**
Where there is no floor beneath, maintenance is planned in advance: a lowering assembly, a
technical platform, or an agreed procedure with a lift. The weight of the composition and
the number of suspension points are known before the order — they determine what is
possible here.
