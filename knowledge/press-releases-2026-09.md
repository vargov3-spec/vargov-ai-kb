## 1. MUSE Design Awards 2026 — Gold Winner (LC0564)

**Самый свежий инфоповод, начинать с него.**

> **Vargov® Design Wins Gold at the MUSE Design Awards 2026 for "Oceanic Illumination"**
> *Russian lighting brand recognised in the Designer / Custom Lighting category*
>
> MOSCOW, September 2026 — Vargov® Design has been named a **Gold Winner** at the **MUSE Design Awards 2026** in the category Lighting Design — Designer / Custom Lighting, for the wall composition **"Oceanic Illumination" (LC0564)**.
>
> The piece is a composition about living matter. A band of mirrored gold with ragged edges is stitched with cells — the skeleton of a sea sponge; inside the cells sit milky bubbles, like air caught in rock. It seems the structure is still growing on the wall. The light is its breathing: it burns inside and comes out through the openings. By day the composition reads as a gold relief with milky inserts; in the evening the openings become the light source and the wall around it takes on the glow.
>
> The composition works singly as a concentrated accent or in a series — and then the coral spreads across the wall as a colony. It is intended for entrance halls, living rooms and spa areas.

## 2. NY Product Design Awards 2023 — Product Designer of the Year (LC0343)

**Самый сильный титул из всех: это звание года, а не степень за изделие.**

> **Anton Vargov Named Product Designer of the Year at the NY Product Design Awards**
> *The title was awarded for the lighting composition LC0343, which also won at the BLT Built Design Awards*
>
> MOSCOW, September 2026 — Anton Vargov, founder and lead designer of Vargov® Design, holds the title of **Product Designer of the Year** at the **NY Product Design Awards 2023**, in the category Lamps & Luminaires — Pendant Luminaires. The entry was the lighting composition **LC0343**, which in the same year was also named **Winner** at the **BLT Built Design Awards 2023** in Electrical & Lighting Systems.
>
> LC0343 is a pond lifted to the ceiling. The bowls open upward rather than down — and that inverts the usual logic: the light here does not pour onto the table, it lies in the leaves like water after rain. The green mass works as a filter: light passes through it and tints the air beneath the composition a soft grass tone. The white bowls keep the colour from going monotonous — they hold the pause.
>
> The composition is intended for a position above a round or oval table, in a winter garden, a spa area, or a living room facing a garden — where the green outside picks up the green of the piece.
