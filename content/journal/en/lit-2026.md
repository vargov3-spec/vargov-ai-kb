# A second win at the LIT Lighting Design Awards

Category: Recognition · Date: 26 November 2026 · Slug: lit-2026

The LC0564 composition has been named Winner in Designer / Custom Lighting. It is our second win at LIT — the first came in 2022, in the same category.

The LIT Lighting Design Awards jury has named the winners of the 2026 season. Among them is our wall composition LC0564 — Winner in Designer / Custom Lighting. The winner's page is open on the award's website: it names the studio, Vargov Design, the lead designer, Anton Vargov, and the category.

What this award is

The LIT Lighting Design Awards is an international lighting-design prize based in Switzerland. It recognises work in three fields: lighting products, architectural lighting and entertainment lighting. Entries are judged by a jury of industry practitioners, academics and members of the trade press.

In 2026 the award turned ten, and the anniversary was marked in June with a ceremony in Paris. According to the organisers, the anniversary season drew 1,022 entries from 63 countries.

The named certificate is signed by the award's leadership. It sits in the awards section together with the 2026 winner's mark.

Why this composition

LC0564 is a composition about the underwater world. A gold openwork frame with an uneven, torn outline recalls the skeleton of a sea sponge; its cells hold milky bubbles, like air stopped on its way to the surface. The structure seems to keep growing right on the wall.

By day it is a gold relief with milky inserts, read through its silhouette and the dense weave of its cells. In the evening the light inside escapes through the openings in warm patches and sharp gold glints, and the relief grows deeper.

It is the composition's second award this year: in August LC0564 took Gold Winner at the MUSE Design Awards 2026.

A second win at LIT

The brand has been recognised at LIT before. In 2022 the LC0223 composition won in the same category — then called Designer and Custom Lighting — and in 2025 the LC0447 composition received an Honorable Mention. The 2026 win is the second: an honourable mention does not count as a win.

What this category means

Designer / Custom Lighting covers authored light and pieces assembled for a specific place rather than produced as a run of identical items. LC0564 works on its own, as an accent, or in a series — then the cells spread across the wall like a colony; the size and the number of elements are chosen for the space.
