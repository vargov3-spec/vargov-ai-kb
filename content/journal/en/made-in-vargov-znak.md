# Made in Vargov

Category: Philosophy · Date: 19 April 2026 · Slug: made-in-vargov-znak

The logo on an LED socket is not a decorative gesture but a principle: an object must carry its identity at every level, even where it is not immediately visible.

We put the Vargov®Design logo on the LED socket not as a decorative gesture, but as the continuation of a principle: an object should carry its identity at every level — not only where you see it straight away.

It is the principle by which the whole object is made.

Even where most would leave nothing but function, we add identity.

It is protection of the original, a signature code, a mark of provenance and respect for our own product, all at once.

At first glance, a small detail. But details like these are exactly what set the true level of a brand.
