# Certificate of authenticity

Category: Authenticity · Date: 3 September 2024 · Slug: sertifikat-podlinnosti

Every piece ships with a certificate of authenticity personally signed by Anton Vargov — proof of authorship and originality in a market of imitations.

Every piece that leaves the Vargov®Design workshop is more than an element of décor or lighting. It is a singular work of art — the sum of creativity, craft and advanced technology. We understand how much it matters to our clients to be certain that what they have bought is authentic and exclusive. That is why, at the buyer's request, we issue a certificate of authenticity confirming the authorship and the originality of the piece.

In a world where copies and fakes turn up more and more often, a certificate of authenticity has become an inseparable part of buying an authored piece. The document does more than confirm that you own an original: it is a sign of your contribution to art and of your support for genuine creative work.

For the certificates we use a special designer paper, high in quality and beautiful in itself. It underlines the singularity of the document and makes it a worthy companion to our work. Every piece that receives a certificate is signed by hand, in ink, by the author. That is not a formality — it is the author personally vouching for the authenticity and the quality of the work.

To receive a certificate of authenticity, simply say so when you place your order. We will gladly prepare this document for you: not only proof of the authenticity of your purchase, but a mark of your refined taste and your commitment to exceptional, exclusive work.

In choosing Vargov®Design you are choosing quality, singularity and genuine creative work. A certificate of authenticity is your way of knowing that what you own is a true work of art — one that will please you and give your interior its character for many years to come.
