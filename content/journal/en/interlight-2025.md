# Interlight 2025

Category: Exhibitions · Date: 26 October 2025 · Slug: interlight-2025

The brand's first exhibition in Russia: a live show of Vargov®Design light, a dialogue with architects, designers and procurement specialists — and one of the most talked-about stands of the fair.

✨ INTERLIGHT 2025 — thank you for being there with us

For us it was the first exhibition in Russia. And it turned out to be far more than a stand with lighting compositions.

For the first time we showed the light of Vargov®Design in person — and received priceless reactions, conversation and support. Architects, designers, procurement specialists, partners — thank you for coming, for looking, for asking, for feeling.

We don't make a mass-market product — we make an object that holds an idea, a silence and a form. And it mattered enormously to see that it resonates.

The company's stand drew the attention of architects, designers, procurement specialists and media not only through its visual presence, but through the particular atmosphere the brand's lighting compositions created.

The architecture of the stand:

A minimal black frame became the backdrop for more than ten signature compositions, assembled by hand from glass, crystal, porcelain and metal.

Floating forms, the intricate geometry of the suspensions, reflections and refractions — every model made you want not simply to photograph it, but to stay and look closer.

Here light did not perform a function — it created a state.

Exactly as intended:

“We don't compare ourselves to anyone. We create within our own system of coordinates. One where light is not a tool but a material. Where form gives birth to a feeling rather than merely illuminating it,” said the brand's founder, Anton Vargov, in an interview.

Real conversation, real interest:

Hundreds of professional contacts, dozens of meetings, discussions, pieces of feedback.

For many visitors this was their first encounter with Vargov®Design — and it stayed with them at once.

It matters to us that light is perceived not as a commodity, but as an object with depth.

And that is exactly the response we got.

What comes next?

Interlight 2025 was a point of entry. Not a conclusion.

The brand will go on developing project supply in Russia through its official representative — xprojectlight.ru.

And it will go on expanding the collections, experimenting with materials and making light that means something.

Until next time. We are already preparing what follows.
