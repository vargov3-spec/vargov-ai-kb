# The design code of Vargov®Design

Category: Philosophy · Date: 15 May 2026 · Slug: dizain-kod

At the heart of the brand is not a visual style but an author's way of seeing form, light and space. Form is born from observation: of natural plasticity, architectural rhythm and the behaviour of light inside the material.

The design code of Vargov®Design cannot be looked at separately from the person behind it.

Because at the heart of the brand lies not a visual style but an author's way of seeing form, light and space.

The founder and chief designer of the brand is Anton Vargov.

It is his artistic thinking that shapes the inner system by which Vargov®Design objects are recognised without a signature.

For Anton Vargov, form never exists for the sake of outward effect.

It is born of observation:

— of the plasticity of nature

— of architectural rhythm

— of the way light behaves inside a material

— of how a single line can hold the tension of an entire composition.

Every object passes through the author's own selection, where only what is truly necessary remains.

No decorative accident.

No visual noise.

No form for form's sake.

“True recognisability begins where a form can no longer be repeated by chance.

An outer silhouette can be copied.

The inner logic of a form — almost never. That is why a strong authorial language protects itself even without a signature”

Vargov®Design — made in light
