# A quest from Vargov®Design

Category: Stories · Date: 20 August 2025 · Slug: kvest

During a shoot at the Hurawalhi Maldives hotel, glass element LC0371 was filmed right in the water on an Indian Ocean reef — and not everything went to plan.

Sometimes the most unusual stories happen by accident ✨

During a shoot at the Hurawalhi Maldives hotel I was filming the glass element LC0371 right in the water, on the reef, in the Indian Ocean 🌊. Beautiful light, glinting waves, living texture — everything was coming together perfectly. And then, at some point, the element slipped out of my hands and… slowly sank to the bottom 🐟 (that moment is in the video).

Instead of getting upset, I decided to turn it into a quest 🎯 — an experiment with format and a continuation of our idea of "light as emotion and story".

We know the exact coordinates of the spot 📍

And I want to announce a contest: whoever finds this element first will receive an author's light composition made of 100 elements like it 💡

I can already hear the comments: "why not hide it on the Moon while you're at it" 😅

Honestly — this was not staged and it is not a prank. It simply happened. And I liked this unexpected format.

How to take part:

1️⃣ Find the LC0371 element at the coordinates given.

2️⃣ Take a photo with your find and tag @vargov_design in a story and in a post.

📍 The coordinates of the spot where the "ray" element lies at a depth of about 4 metres: 5.5217537, 73.4377352

👉 View on Google Maps
