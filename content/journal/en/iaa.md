# International Awards Associate (IAA)

Category: Recognition · Date: 4 September 2024 · Slug: iaa

The IAA announced Anton Vargov as a juror of the NY Product Design Awards, MUSE Design Awards, Rome Design Awards, London Design Awards, French Design Awards and more.

International Awards Associate (IAA) has announced the appointment of Anton Vargov to the juries of the prestigious NY Product Design Awards, MUSE Design Awards, Rome Design Awards, London Design Awards, French Design Awards, TITAN Property Awards and Noble World Hotel Awards

“It is a genuine honour for me. I am glad to be able to contribute to the judging of outstanding work in design. These awards are an important platform for recognising and encouraging innovation in design, and I am proud to have the chance to take part in that process. For me it is a chance not only to assess the work of others, but to be part of shaping the design trends of the future”
