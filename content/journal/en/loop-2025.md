Vargov®Design honoured at the international LOOP Design Awards 2025

Vargov®Design has received international recognition: the light composition LC0458 is among the winners of the prestigious LOOP Design Awards 2025.

About the LOOP Design Awards

The LOOP Design Awards are an international competition that gathers architecture and design projects from all over the world every year. The jury brings together leading experts in the field, and taking part is considered an important step for designers and architects who want to be heard on a global level.

To be named a winner at the LOOP Design Awards is to confirm not only the quality of the work, but its relevance to the international professional community.

The LC0458 light composition

LC0458 embodies the philosophy of Vargov®Design: contemporary design joined with an artist's approach to glass and light.

The main elements are made of textured glass in the shape of half-circles that create a singular play of light and shadow.

Integrated LEDs light the surface softly, bringing out the depth of the texture.

The composition comes in different configurations — from the intimate to the large-scale — which makes it equally at home in residential and public interiors.

The symbolism of the half-circle points to images from nature, the sun and the moon, giving the piece a universal quality and a particular expressiveness.

What the award means

For Vargov®Design, winning the LOOP Design Awards 2025 is not simply a title but confirmation that the path we chose is the right one. Our philosophy is to see in light not only a function but an emotion, and to turn a technical object into a work of art.

International recognition shows that this approach resonates far beyond our own borders and has its place in the global conversation about design.

In closing

Vargov®Design continues to develop its own line of author's light compositions, bringing together technology, aesthetics and work done by hand. Winning the LOOP Design Awards 2025 is an important milestone in our story and a reason to keep going.
