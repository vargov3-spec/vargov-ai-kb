# Gold at the MUSE Design Awards 2026

Category: Recognition · Date: 31 August 2026 · Slug: muse-2026

The LC0564 composition «Oceanic Illumination» has been named Gold Winner in Lighting Design — Designer / Custom Lighting.

Vargov®Design is a Gold Winner of the MUSE Design Awards 2026. The prize went to the sculptural wall composition LC0564 «Oceanic Illumination» in the Lighting Design — Designer / Custom Lighting category.

About the MUSE Design Awards

The MUSE Design Awards is an international design competition run by the New York-based International Awards Associate (IAA). Entries are assessed by a jury drawn from several countries, and awards are given in tiers: platinum, gold, silver and bronze.

The work itself is assessed — the idea, the execution and how well the object solves the task set for it; the panel brings together architects, interior and product designers, creative directors. That is why gold in a narrow category of bespoke lighting counts for more than a general certificate.

«Oceanic Illumination», composition LC0564

A composition about living matter. A ribbon of mirrored gold with torn edges is pierced by cells — the skeleton of a sea sponge; milky bubbles sit inside them like air caught in the rock. The structure seems to keep growing right on the wall.

Light is its breathing: it burns inside and comes out through the openings. By day the piece reads as a gold relief with milky inlays, tactile and intricate. In the evening the light escapes in warm patches and sharp golden glints — the coral starts to glow through its pores.

The composition works alone as a concentrated art accent, or in a series — then the coral spreads across the wall as a colony. Size, cell configuration and the number of elements are matched to the particular space.

A second MUSE award

This is the brand's second recognition at MUSE: in 2023 the LC0237 composition took platinum in the same category. Both entries are open on the award's own site, with the piece, category and tier stated — so the award can be checked rather than taken on trust.

In closing

For Vargov®Design an award is not a title but a bearing check: confirmation that bespoke light, assembled by hand for a particular interior, reads well beyond its home market.
