# SIT Award 2025

Category: Recognition · Date: 28 May 2025 · Slug: sit-2025

Lighting composition LC0358 took a winning place at the international SIT Furniture Design Award 2025.

My light composition LC0358 has been awarded a prize at the SIT Design Award 2025!

I am glad to share the news: one of my projects has taken a prize at the prestigious international SIT Furniture Design Award. It is an important recognition of my work and of my ideas in product design.

The project is already featured in the official winners' gallery on the award's website and will be included in the annual SIT Award catalogue.

Thank you to the SIT Design Award team for this recognition and for the support they give the design community. And to everyone who follows what we do — thank you for your faith and your inspiration.

The light composition LC0358 combines the delicacy of its glass elements with the dynamic functionality of an adjustable metal frame. Textured glass discs are its heart, complemented by integrated LED lighting.

Description:

Materials and construction:

Glass elements: textured glass with an iridescent effect that creates a refined play of light and shadow.

Metal frame: sturdy metal with an elegant gold finish. The adjustable frame lets you set the direction of the light and the position of the glass elements.

Lighting:

Integrated LED sources give a soft, diffused light that brings out the texture of the glass.

The light picks out every detail, creating a warm atmosphere and visual depth.

Dynamic, flexible design:

The adjustable metal frame allows the angle and position of the glass discs to be changed, adapting the composition to different spaces and moods.

Available in vertical, horizontal and spiral configurations, which makes the model highly versatile.

Design and aesthetics:

The organic shapes and textured materials are inspired by nature — by leaves, by water and by their movement.
