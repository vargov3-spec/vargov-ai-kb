# Product texts — batch 3: closing the gaps (LC0586, LC0593, LC0595, LC0597)

English version. Approved structure: artistic image and intent → the shift from day to evening → **Where it works.** → **Style.** → made-to-order parameters. Materials are not named.

---

## LC0586 — Driplight floor lamp

A composition about a joke told in earnest. Driplight repeats the silhouette of a medical drip stand: a polished pole, a fine tube, a control switch and, in place of the infusion bag, a matte shade. The irony is obvious, but the message is serious: support, recovery, an inner reserve — the things a fast pace of life is always short of.

By day it is a sculptural object with a recognisable silhouette: a clean pole, an exact proportion, a piece read through form and quotation. In the evening the Driplight "drips" a soft warm light — the best metaphor for recovery a piece of furniture can permit itself.

For all its conceptual bent this is a fully functional floor lamp: 150–170 cm tall, a stable base, a dimmable 8–12 W source — enough light to read by in the armchair beside it.

**Where it works.** Bold interiors, design spaces, the lounge areas of clinics and studios, private living rooms. The piece needs a viewer who gets the quotation.

**Style.** Conceptual design and postmodernism: an object as a statement. The message is ironic and humane — a piece for someone who values meaning as much as form.

The height of the pole, the finish and the colour temperature of the light are all made to order.

## LC0593 — Floor lamp

A composition about a bouquet in which the light has opened. From the base rises a bundle of golden stems, and on each a pleated parasol unfurls — a form that reads as a giant water-lily pad, as a flower head and as a sea fan.

The parasols stand at differing heights: a bouquet, not a formation.

By day it is fine botany on gold stems: the corrugation reads through light and shade, and the deep green velvet of the base gives the piece weight and warmth. In the evening light comes on in the gold core of every parasol, rays radiate along the folds, and each disc acquires a halo of its own — a soft shadow below, a glowing relief above. A small garden of light beside an armchair.

**Where it works.** Beside an armchair, beside a bed, in a private living room, in a boudoir. Several blooms at differing heights replace both a floor lamp and a night light — the light is adjusted by tilting the parasols.

**Style.** Art Nouveau and contemporary classic: botany with velvet and gilding. The message is intimate and warm — a piece for a private space.

The number and tilt of the parasols, the finish of the stems and the colour of the base are all made to order.

## LC0595 — Table art object

A composition about a dialogue between the wild and the perfect. The base is an unworked black stone with chipped facets, but on its cut face a honeycomb pattern is carved: as though the rock had begun turning into ornament by itself. Above the stone, at an angle, sits a disc with the same relief.

The pattern of the stone picks up the pattern of the disc: the unworked and the perfect turn out to share one nature. Every object is unique — the stone of the base is never repeated.

By day it is a sculpture on a table: the relief reads through light and shade, the chips of the stone through texture, a tactile and complex piece. In the evening light is born at the golden centre and spreads through the cells: the matte disc glows evenly, like a moon; the faceted one breaks the light into hundreds of highlights. A pair is especially expressive — a matte one and a clear one side by side.

**Where it works.** A console, a mantelpiece, a bedside table, a desk. The compact format (300 × 220 mm, 440 mm tall) gathers a surface around it without needing any ceiling space.

**Style.** Wabi-sabi and natural minimalism: the fracture and the ornament in one piece. The message is contemplative — a piece for the inner circle.

The execution of the disc, the character of the stone and the finish of the centre are all made to order.

## LC0597 — Light composition

A composition about the elements. A soft undulating lower edge makes the silhouette alive: at the bottom the composition does not break off, it runs down — banners in the wind, weed in the body of water, an aurora arrested in a room.

Plates of two characters, matte white and clear coloured, descend from the ceiling on fine cables; above them a scattering of miniature points holds the curtain in the air, like stars.

By day, as you move through the room, the layers reassemble and the drawing never repeats: in a cold register it is ice and turquoise, in a deep green one a dark field with a gold reverse. In the evening the points above light up, and the green version becomes an evening forest with the sun burning deep inside it, the cold one a radiance over water.

**Where it works.** Lobbies, atriums, double-height living rooms, stairwells, restaurants. Related to the LC0598 series: the same plates, a different character — a city there, the elements here.

**Style.** Contemporary sculptural installation and natural modern: the curtain as a form. The message is atmospheric — a piece that changes the state of a space.

The dimensions, the density, the palette and the drawing of the lower edge are all made to order.
