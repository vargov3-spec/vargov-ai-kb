## LC0490 — Light composition

A composition about the phases of a wave. Every element is arrested in its own: crest, fall, run-up — together they give not a drawing but the storyboard of a single movement.

White gathers the mass into one cloud: strict, architectural, almost graphic. The light comes from the bends rather than from above.

By day it is an even white field overhead — a piece read through the plasticity of the bends and soft chiaroscuro, calm and uniform. In the evening light comes out of every bend and the field begins to shimmer like a sea under a low sun: the crests burn, the falls stay dark.

**Where it works.** Large spaces — lobbies, conference halls, retail galleries, panoramic living rooms. The composition covers a hall in an even layer of light and movement: it works as the main lighting.

**Style.** Parametric design and contemporary architecture: an array instead of an object. The message is monumental — a piece that is a surface, for a building.

The area of the field, the number of waves and the density are all made to order.

## LC0489 — Light composition

A composition about a current stopped mid-movement. Every ribbon has a plasticity of its own: here a wave, there stretched cloth, there a jet of air. There are no two alike, because a current does not repeat itself.

In the mass there are hundreds of air bubbles; gold clasps catch the ribbon along its whole length. The fine suspensions dissolve into the dark, and the columns hover.

By day it is a clear forest of verticals: the bubbles read against the light, the ribbons as smooth waves, a piece light and almost invisible. In the evening light enters through the clasps and ignites the bubbles — the mass fills with sparks and the columns become luminous right through.

**Where it works.** Staircases, double-height living rooms, window displays, atriums. The forest of verticals is designed for a specific height and reads from several levels.

**Style.** Contemporary sculptural installation: the movement of water as a motif. The message is light and fluid — a piece for an interior that avoids stasis.

The number of ribbons, their heights and the density of the forest are all made to order.

## LC0488 — Decorative composition

A composition with no light of its own. The piece works exclusively on borrowed light: the gloss and the gold border gather a room's illumination and shift with every step. A sculpture made of highlights that needs no wiring.

The elements are bent like a petal in the wind or a sweet in an opened wrapper; the cascade is triangular, narrowing downward, the edge traced in gold.

By day the cascade lives on daylight from the window: the white gloss reflects it softly, the gold border sharply, and the drawing of highlights changes as the sun moves. In the evening directed lamplight makes the cascade flare entire — the piece catches fire the more strongly the harder you light it from the side.

**Where it works.** Grand staircases, entrance halls, the zone above a round table. The composition gives volume and luxury with no lighting technology — suitable where wiring is impossible but directed light is available nearby.

**Style.** Hollywood glamour and contemporary Art Deco: shine as the content. The message is dressy — a piece for a space that wants an effect.

The number of elements, the shape of the cascade and the share of gold are all made to order.

## LC0487 — Light composition

A composition about lace turned by the sea. The organic perforation in the white plates reads as coral lace — a pattern nobody drew: the water left it.

Between the layers, coloured butterflies flit — blue, amber, gold. The colour is metered like a brooch on a white dress: the composition is festive without being gaudy.

By day it is a three-dimensional white cloud with occasional coloured accents: the perforation reads as graphic work, the butterflies as points of colour. In the evening light burns inside the lace and shows through the openings as hundreds of warm points, while the butterflies' wings pick it up and flare — a white field in which precious details have lit up.

**Where it works.** Living rooms, bedrooms, children's rooms, wedding halls, boutiques. The layered cloud requires height — pressed to the ceiling, it loses its depth.

**Style.** Art Nouveau and marine modern: lace and butterfly as paired motifs. The message is festive and tender — a piece for a light interior.

The number of plates, the mix of butterflies and the scale are all made to order.

## LC0486 — Decorative composition

A composition about an underwater landscape dissolved in air. The piece lights less than it creates depth: curved plates swim in a funnel through the body of the space, and above them shimmers a scattering of white points — plankton near the surface.

The finest drawing of coral veining on the turquoise and smoky plates reads close up and disappears at a distance.

By day it is a calm turquoise-and-smoke mass: the colour reads against the light, and the composition creates a sense of depth in daylight already. In the evening the points above the funnel flare as suspension, the plates go into shadow, and a body of water appears in the room where only what is nearer the surface can be seen.

**Where it works.** Spas, pools, bathrooms, interiors in a marine register. The composition is atmospheric rather than working — separate functional light is needed with it.

**Style.** Marine modern and bio-design: coral and plankton as motifs. The message is contemplative — a piece for a space where people unwind.

The number of plates, the balance of turquoise to smoke and the dimensions are all made to order.

## LC0485 — Light composition

A composition about water in a frame. A strict contour and free plasticity within — a formula that makes the object architectural and alive at once: the frame holds, the wave moves.

Clear ribbons with a bubbled mass roll inside the frame, and glowing strips light every crest.

By day, from below, the composition reads as the surface of water raised above the table: the bubbles are visible against the light, the crests catch light from the window. In the evening light lifts every crest from within, and the water begins to move before your eyes — the frame stays still while the contents live.

**Where it works.** Above a long table, a kitchen island, a reception desk. The linear format is calculated for the length of the zone; the light is directed downward and gives working illumination.

**Style.** Contemporary minimalism and architectural design: the frame as a device. The message is rational and beautiful at once — a piece for an interior that loves order.

The length, the number of waves and the finish of the frame are all made to order.

## LC0484 — Floor lamp — light sculpture

The same wave stood upright. Clear blocks are assembled into a strict column inside which a fluid form rolls: strict outside, alive within.

The light does not come out directly — it slides along the bends, refracts and fills the mass with depth.

By day it is a clear column with a bubbled texture: a piece read through distortions and inner drawing, almost without mass. In the evening the light reveals the depth — the mass fills and the wave inside becomes visible entire. A black frame with turquoise light gives a nocturnal, electric reading; a gold one with warm light an intimate, jewel-like one.

**Where it works.** The corner of a living room, a study, a bedroom, a reception room. The vertical takes little floor area and works both as a source and as a sculpture.

**Style.** Contemporary minimalism and optical art: refraction as the content. The message is technical and mesmerising — a piece for an interior with contemporary taste.

The height, the finish of the frame and the colour of the light are all made to order.

## LC0483 — Light composition

A composition about an oyster with fire instead of a pearl. White valves open in pairs, and between them hang amber "mussels" — what the shell is opened for has been replaced with light.

The clusters gather into a free cloud: the whiteness gives a matte rhythm, the amber warm flares in the depth.

By day it is white valves with a wavy relief and amber bodies between them — a piece read through the plasticity of the shell, a marine theme kept from the literal. In the evening the amber "mussels" light from within while the valves stay matte: the light comes not from everywhere but from the depth of every pair. A precious find from the shore.

**Where it works.** Living rooms, spa areas, restaurants, reception rooms, interiors beside water. The free cloud is assembled for a specific plan.

**Style.** Marine Art Nouveau and Mediterranean classicism: the shell as a symbol of hidden value. The message is warm — a piece for an interior in a light register.

The number of valves and inner elements and the scale of the cloud are all made to order.

## LC0482 — Light composition

A composition about equilibrium. A disc on a fine needle axis — a spinning top stopped in perfect balance: a position that in reality lasts fractions of a second.

The swarm hovers at differing heights; the monochrome makes it light and strict at once: the composition plays on light and form alone, without one colour.

By day it is clear parasols on needles — a piece read through silhouettes and ribbing, light and almost graphic. In the evening light burns in the axis beneath the disc, and the ribbed surface drives it round in a silver radiance: every top acquires a glowing halo, and the swarm begins to seem to be turning.

**Where it works.** Contemporary spaces — above a table, in a meeting room, in an entrance hall, in a lobby. The flat discs steal no height: the composition suits medium ceilings too.

**Style.** Contemporary minimalism and optical art: rotation as subject. The message is technical and playful — a piece for an interior of clean geometry.

The number of tops, their calibres and their heights are all made to order.

## LC0481 — Light composition

A composition about wealth hidden inside. Stone outside, sun within: the split geodes show a dark mercury surface on the outside and a hot gold in the break. The device comes from nature — that is how real geodes are built, and everything of value in them opens only after the split.

Every element is unique: its own break, its own relief, its own drawing of the melt.

By day it is dark mirrored stones on copper rods — a piece read through reflections, with the gold interior visible only from certain positions. In the evening light comes out of the breaks in warm flashes and reflects in the dark facets of the neighbours: the stones begin to wink at each other while the mass around stays dark.

**Where it works.** Private living rooms, studies, bars, lounges, showrooms. The dark mass requires dark surroundings: against a pale wall the contrast stops working.

**Style.** Brutal opulence and natural aesthetics: the mineral as it is. The message is strong and collectorly — a piece for an interior with character.

The number of geodes, their sizes and the composition of the swarm are all made to order.
