# Product texts — LC0599–LC0602

English version. Approved structure: artistic image and intent → the shift from day to evening → **Where it works.** → **Style.** → made-to-order parameters. Materials are not named.

---

## LC0599 — Light composition

A composition about a stained-glass window taken out of a wall. The engraving repeats the architecture of old cathedrals: lancet windows, openwork façades, round rose windows, spires reaching upward. Every plate is a window released into the air.

Elements in four heights are composed freely: a dense vertical cascade, a horizontal field beneath the ceiling, or a gallery of hovering windows along a wall.

By day it is graphic work — a fine engraved drawing on a clear field, read against the light. In the evening a point of light above every plate passes through the engraving and the drawing begins to glow from within: an architecture of light, a city of cathedrals suspended in the air.

**Where it works.** Spaces with high ceilings, stairwells, lofts, lobbies — wherever light should tell a story. The plates are produced in amber-gold, deep turquoise or graphite-clear — from warm intimacy to Gothic coolness.

**Style.** Gothic in a contemporary reading and Art Deco: architectural ornament kept from the literal. The message is narrative — a piece for an interior that loves meaning.

The number of plates, the drop heights and the tone are all made to order.

## LC0600 — Wall and ceiling decorative composition

A composition in two layers. The first is the hemispheres themselves with their engraved coral pattern: branching, cellular, radial structures, no two drawings alike. The second is the shadow: under a point source the engraving throws a second, shadow composition onto the wall and ceiling, and the drawing begins to live apart from the object.

The convex surface holds the pattern in three dimensions, and it reads differently from every position.

By day it is graphic art turned object: clear, powdery pink, blue, turquoise and smoky bronze hemispheres with the finest white line — an oceanic collection gathered not in an album but in space. In the evening the second layer comes on, and a shadow repeating the pattern appears on the wall — the object doubles.

**Where it works.** An intimate group above a console or a continuous coral field across a whole wall; on a ceiling the composition opens like a flowering above water — in spa areas and beside a pool it is reflected and doubles once more.

**Style.** Marine modern and the graphic school: the drawing as the content. The message is collectorly — a piece for an interior that is examined.

The make-up of the hemispheres, their diameters and their tones are all made to order.

## LC0601 — Light composition

A composition about a flock at dawn. The birds are lit from below — the way they look at the hour when the sun is still under the horizon and the light comes from the ground rather than the sky. That one device sets the whole time of day inside the piece.

Every figure is cast at a moment of movement: spread wings with plumage in relief, a body that has caught a rising current. There is no prescribed scheme — the birds line up in a wedge, scatter into a free flock, rise in a spiral or draw out into a train.

By day it is a sculpture in the air: a white matte finish and warm golden metal keep the composition in a noble, calm register, and the piece reads through the plasticity of the plumage. In the evening the sources under the wings come on and the flock carries the light — a dawn that arrives on schedule.

**Where it works.** Double-height living rooms, staircases, hotel lobbies, galleries. The same series becomes an intimate group above a table or an installation tens of metres long.

**Style.** Art Nouveau and contemporary sculpture: flight as a classic motif. The message is poetic — a piece that stops a moment.

The number of birds, the trajectory of the flight and the height and direction of each are all made to order.

## LC0602 — Floor lamp

The same flock in a floor format. Around a vertical pole the birds circle in a rising spiral, and the eye reads the movement from the bottom up: the flock takes off and the light rises with it.

The light here is not a function but company: it stands beside you like something alive.

By day the floor lamp works as a sculpture: the fine plasticity of the plumage, a clean golden vertical, the rhythm of the wings. In the evening it works as a source of soft, living light that does not flood but is scattered along the silhouette, like windows at dusk.

**Where it works.** A living room, a bedroom, a study, a reception room. It is an independent object and at the same time a continuation of the LC0601 series: a suspended flock beneath the ceiling and a rising flock beside an armchair gather a space into one story.

**Style.** Art Nouveau and contemporary sculpture: the vertical as a trajectory of take-off. The message is lyrical — a piece for an interior that loves stories.

The number of birds, their placement on the pole and the finish are all made to order.
