## LC0598 — Light composition

A composition about an evening horizon. Hundreds of narrow plates on cables of differing length form the silhouette of a city: setbacks, towers, gaps, light in the windows. Inside each is a matte drawing, as though branches or currents had set in the mass.

The composition never looks the same twice: as you move around it the plates reassemble — a dense wall from one position, transparent lacework from another.

By day it is the graphic of silhouettes read through overlay and inner drawing; the same form in emerald, crystal white and amber sounds like a northern forest, a winter city or a sunset. In the evening points of warm light scatter between the plates like lights in windows, and the horizon catches fire.

**Where it works.** A cascade above a living room, an extended wave along a staircase, a dense cloud in an entrance hall. The shape is assembled for the space and reads from a distance.

**Style.** Urban modernism and contemporary sculpture: the city as a motif. The message is narrative — a piece that sets the mood of a whole room.

The size and shape of the composition and the number and colour of the elements are all made to order.

## LC0596 — Light composition

A composition about a quiet dialogue. Discs of two characters — matte white with radial pleating and clear with a faceted radial drawing — hold a conversation in which neither shouts the other down.

At the centre of each is a golden accent that gathers the disc the way a button gathers cloth.

By day the composition works through texture: relief, facets, the warm glimmer of the cores; the cloud opens slowly as you walk around it — behind a matte disc a clear one flares, and behind that another matte one. In the evening the roles diverge: the matte ones hold a soft shadow, the clear ones light from within, and the cloud shimmers like a bloom of jellyfish in dark water.

**Where it works.** Living rooms, dining rooms, spa areas, meeting rooms, lobbies. The layering reads from below and from the side: the composition is not hung higher than necessary.

**Style.** Contemporary classic and marine modern: the disc as a pure form. The message is calm — a piece for an interior that loves a nuance.

The size, the density and the ratio of matte to clear discs are all made to order.

## LC0594 — Wall light compositions

A composition about reversed roles. By day it is a sculptural panel: a play of relief, matte surface and mirrored facets in which the white discs lead, with a deep carving radiating from the centre like the petals of a chrysanthemum. In the evening the roles change: the faceted ones flare with gold haloes, the matte ones recede into soft chiaroscuro, and the wall turns into a night sky with suns in it.

Round elements in two diameters gather on a wall as a free constellation — a scattering rather than a grid.

**Where they work.** From a strict pair above a console to a field scattered up the full height of a double-height wall — entrance halls, living rooms, stair landings, reception desks. The layout of the constellation is chosen for a specific wall.

**Style.** Art Deco and contemporary sculpture: the disc as a module. The message is decorative and flexible — a piece that adapts to any wall.

The number of elements, their combination and the dimensions of the panel are all made to order.

## LC0592 — Decorative composition

A composition about cartography. Inside every plate is a labyrinth of relief lines: the veins of a leaf, the meanders of river beds, the contour rings on an old map. Three readings of one drawing, and all three about how terrain is put together.

Elements with a live uneven contour hover in layers: the composition reads differently from every position — now floating leaves, now an archipelago, now the section of a fantastical landform.

By day it is a transparent sculpture in the air: the deep green reads against the light and the drawing is visible in the mass. In the evening light passes through the plates and carries the drawing outward — green shadows and highlights settle on the walls and begin to live as a separate graphic, and the labyrinths of lines turn into a glowing map.

**Where it works.** Living rooms, winter gardens, lobbies, restaurants, studies. The coloured shadows on the walls are part of the effect: quiet surfaces are needed nearby.

**Style.** Natural modern and cartographic aesthetics: the line as the content. The message is intellectual — a piece for an interior that loves detail.

The shape of the cloud, the number of layers and elements and the tone are all made to order.

## LC0591 — Light composition

A composition about a line drawn in the air. Curved clear tubes join into continuous trajectories — loops, orbits, strokes; every composition becomes a gesture of its own: one lies as a calm wave, another twists into an interlacing of orbits, a third hangs above a table as a calligraphic signature.

This is a long exposure of light, arrested in the frame.

By day it is a transparent sculpture with warm golden joints — a piece read through trajectory and with almost no mass. In the evening the light flows inside the tubes like a current of energy, and pure graphic work remains: the lines are reflected in windows and polished stone, doubling through the space.

**Where it works.** From a compact stroke above a table to an installation across a whole hall — living rooms, lobbies, restaurants, showrooms. The drawing is composed for a specific space.

**Style.** Contemporary sculptural installation and calligraphic aesthetics: the line as the only material. The message is artistic — a piece that is a gesture.

The trajectory, the scale and the number of tubes are all made to order.

## LC0590 — Light composition

A composition about honey through a comb. The light passes through the modules densely, slowly, enveloping — it does not pour, it seeps. The device is prompted by the form itself: vertical cells standing side by side.

Assembled, the composition recalls a honeycomb, a druse of crystals and a fragment of set rock at once — a natural structure translated into the language of architecture. The frame holds the form and the rhythm, the light-transmitting part the depth and the warmth.

By day it is a strict geometry with clean facets: a piece read through the rhythm of the cells, almost an architectural detail. In the evening an amber glow from within makes it warm and deep — rock with the sun caught inside it.

**Where it works.** A compact group works as an accent sculpture above a table; a large assembly as a glowing column filling the vertical of a double-height space.

**Style.** Natural architecture and brutal opulence: the cell as a structural module. The message is warm and substantial — a piece for an interior with character.

The number of modules, the height of the assembly and the configuration are all made to order.

## LC0589 — Light composition

A composition about topography. The upper and lower contours of the field change smoothly along its whole length — like contour lines on a map, like a mountain ridge or an ocean current. Landscape translated into light, quite literally.

The object lives by its depth: the elements stand in layers of differing density, and from every viewpoint the relief reassembles.

By day it is a dense field of verticals with a live contour: a piece read through silhouette and layers, monumental and quiet. In the evening hundreds of warm points come on inside the field — the lights of settlements on a night slope. The colour sets the geography: amber for dunes and sandstone, blue for currents and glaciers, green for valleys after rain.

**Where it works.** Lobbies, atriums, conference halls, panoramic living rooms, gallery spaces. The relief is designed anew for every interior.

**Style.** Parametric design and natural aesthetics: the landscape as a form. The message is monumental — a piece that is architecture.

The dimensions of the field, the changes in contour and the density and colour of the elements are all made to order.

## LC0588 — Light composition

A composition about a galaxy of one's own. Planets hang in different orbits, elongated golden elements stitch the space between them like rockets on the launch pad, and the whole system opens slowly as you move through the room.

The round elements read as the surfaces of planets: a relief texture with craters and seas, lit around the edge by a fine ring of light.

By day it is a sculptural installation with a play of matte against mirrored: the relief reads through light and shade, the polished bodies catch the room. In the evening the room goes out and only space remains — glowing planet discs, trajectories, stars.

**Where it works.** Children's rooms, dark evening interiors, lobbies, stairwells, showrooms. The effect works with subdued ambient light.

**Style.** Cosmic modernism and mid-century: the orbit as a motif. The message is fairy-tale and contemporary — a piece for a space that loves stories.

The number of planets and rockets, the diameters and the drop heights are all made to order.

## LC0587 — Light composition

A composition about the cable becoming the hero. It is usually hidden — here hundreds of fine cords descend from the ceiling as a dense stream, each ruling the space with a line of its own.

The lower edge is not level: the drops hang at differing heights, forming a relief of light — rain stopped an instant before the ground.

By day it is live graphic work: close up the individual lines of the cords are visible, from a distance a vertical hatching, almost a draft drawing. In the evening the warm points gather into a dense shimmering cloud that does not dazzle but envelops — a waterfall of light with no visible source.

**Where it works.** The composition can descend through an entire stairwell or hang low above a table, marking out a zone of light. The length of the cords is unlimited.

**Style.** Industrial minimalism and contemporary installation: structure as decor. The message is honest — a piece that does not hide how it is made.

The density of the rain, the area, the differences in height and the number of elements are all made to order.

## LC0585 — Light composition

A composition about a moment caught in a fold. The plasticity of folded cloth is arrested — every element is a small sculpture in which the folds catch light and shadow.

This is a modular system rather than a chandelier in the usual sense: the elements assemble into a ring, a cascade, an elongated line, a dense volume or an almost airy structure. The composition changes with the angle of view and holds its character every time.

By day the white reads through the plasticity of the folds, the dark elements add contrast and depth, and the gold centre turns every module into a point of attraction in its own right. In the evening the light does not flood the form — it comes out from within, like breathing: a soft accent that emphasises the relief and makes the white warm and the dark velvet.

**Where it works.** Living rooms, lobbies, dining rooms, stairwells, reception rooms. The modularity allows the composition to be assembled to any geometry — from a compact ring to an architectural volume.

**Style.** Contemporary sculpture and Art Deco: the fold as a module. The message is flexible and expressive — a piece that is built to the space.

The size, the shape, the number of elements and the balance of light to dark are all made to order.
