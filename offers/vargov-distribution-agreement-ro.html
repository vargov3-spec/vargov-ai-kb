IThe Offer in Brief

Vargov® Design invites your company to become the exclusive distributor of the brand in Romania. Below is the full text of the agreement, ready for discussion and signature. The agreement is trilateral: alongside you, it is signed by the brand owner and by the manufacturing factory — giving you direct access to production and to factory prices with no intermediary markup.

What the distributor receives

  • The status of the official and exclusive representative of Vargov® Design in Romania: the exclusive right to sell, display and promote the brand’s products within the territory.
  • Supply at factory prices with no markup by the brand owner or third parties; direct communication with the factory manager on pricing and lead times — opened once the display order is placed. The distributor sets end-customer prices independently.
  • Freedom of sales within the territory: selling to any buyers — end customers, designers, architects, procurement companies, retail and project organisations — in its own name and on its own commercial terms. Only the brand owner may confer dealer, agent or brand-representative status on third parties.
  • The official brand guide, 3D models, photographs and video materials of the products and of production.
  • Publication on vargov.ru as an official partner — within 7–10 days of signing.
  • A 12-month factory warranty; defective items replaced at the manufacturer’s expense, including re-shipment.
  • Real protection of exclusivity: a ban on direct supplies into the territory, a duty on the brand and the factory to establish the final site of incoming enquiries and refer them to you, a ban on supplies into your territory through the brand’s other partners, a USD 20,000 penalty per breach and a clear claim procedure.

What the brand expects from the distributor

  • A purchase of products worth at least USD 50,000 to create a permanent display — you choose the range and sizes to suit your premises.
  • An operating showroom, designed in the brand’s corporate style and maintained in proper aesthetic and technical condition.
  • Selling from the display with subsequent renewal — display items may be sold without separate consent, provided the brand owner is notified, the fee is paid and the sold item is replaced with a new model.
  • An annual order volume of at least USD 100,000 in production value — the condition for keeping exclusivity. The display order counts towards the first year.
  • A brand fee of 10 % of the contract value agreed with the end customer — paid to the factory’s account together with the payment for the order itself. Installation, delivery and your project services are excluded from the fee base.
  • Compliance with the brand guide, confidentiality, and the ban on copies and analogues.
Why the display is mandatory. A lighting composition does not sell from a photograph: its scale, its light and its build quality register only in person. A distributor with a live display closes the sale on the spot — without waiting out a production cycle. That is the core commercial advantage this partnership is built around.

Termination of the Agreement does not relieve the Parties from their obligations to pay for already agreed deliveries, nor does it terminate the provisions governing intellectual property, confidentiality, liability, applicable law and dispute resolution.
