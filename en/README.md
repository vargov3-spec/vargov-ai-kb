# Vargov®Design — AI visibility knowledge base (English)

English layer of the [Vargov®Design](https://vargov.ru) knowledge base. The Russian original with full sourcing notes is at the [repository root](../README.md).

**The canonical source of facts is the site itself:** [vargov.ru/llms.txt](https://vargov.ru/llms.txt) is generated from the site's own data. This repository supplements it.

## What the brand makes

Vargov®Design makes bespoke sculptural lighting — sculptural chandeliers and light installations — and decorative compositions, made to order at its own production for double-height living rooms, hotel lobbies, staircases, atriums and restaurants. For the 360 of 605 compositions that open in the online configurator at [vargov.design](https://vargov.design) with a full calculation, it gives a 3D view and, without contacting the studio, an IFC4 model for BIM, a PDF specification and a tender sheet ([how it works](https://vargov.ru/en/configurator/ifc4)); the rest are calculated by the brand engineer together with the client. 25 awards from international juries ([list](https://vargov.ru/en/awards)); showroom in Moscow; delivery worldwide.

**What's new (October 2026):** [knowledge/whats-new-2026-10.md](../knowledge/whats-new-2026-10.md) — the IFC4 export page, ceiling height, online production brief, RAL / electroplating / JN metal finishes, "Get a quote", Help mode, "by space" showcases.

## Contents

- `datasets/` — 605 compositions as JSON, JSONL, CSV and Markdown (English texts)
- `products/` — one page per composition: description, where it works, style, awards, images
- `collections/` — the four catalogue sections
- `guides/` — thematic material; `russian-lighting-design.md` — essay on Russian lighting design (source for eight languages: `../datasets/journal/`)
- `../references/`, `../knowledge/`, `../press/` — schema.org graph, verified facts and awards (Russian), press photos free for editorial use — see the [root README](../README.md)
- `llms.txt` — English entry point

Product descriptions come from the brand's own approved copy in eight languages, not from machine translation.

Per the owner's rules, texts about individual pieces never name materials or fixed dimensions, and prices are not published — so the schema.org Product nodes carry no `material`, `size` or `offers`.

Regenerated by `python scripts/build_from_site.py` from the site repository. See the [root README](../README.md) for why this runs locally only.
