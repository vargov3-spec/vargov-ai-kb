## Partnership proposal

### Who this is for

Lighting and interior showrooms, design galleries, project procurement companies and studios that have their own display space and a client flow in the premium interior segment — private residences, hotels, restaurants and public spaces.

### About the brand

**Vargov® Design** is a Russian brand of author's lighting and decorative compositions. Concepts and design are created in Russia; manufacturing takes place at the brand's own factory in China. Trademark VARGOV No. 896936. The catalogue holds 600+ author's compositions across four categories: lighting compositions, decorative compositions, sculptural compositions and decor, floor lamps and tabletop art objects.

Founder and chief designer **Anton Vargov** is Designer of the Year (Gold) at the NY Product Design Awards and a Platinum Winner at the International Architecture & Design Awards, and serves as an expert and jury member for Russian and international design awards. The brand has also been recognised by the International Design Awards, LOOP Design Awards and RLDC. In October 2025 Vargov® Design made its debut at Interlight Moscow, where its stand became one of the most talked-about exhibits.

Brand line: **"Russian design. World scale. Made in Vargov®."**

### What you get as an authorised dealer

1. **Official status and the full catalogue** — authorised dealer status with access to the entire range, including collection pieces.
2. **A transparent financial model and engineering support** — the dealer buys at factory prices with no brand markup and sets the end-customer price freely; the brand's remuneration is 10% of the Contract Value agreed with the end customer, paid to the factory together with the order payment. Plus technical support on projects: 1:1 installation drawings, 12V and 220V versions, assistance during selection and approval.
3. **Export and logistics handled** — timber crating and plywood boxes, a full set of export documents, worldwide delivery.
4. **Speed for procurement teams** — project specification and quotation within 24 hours.
5. **Any size, made to order** — a composition is assembled from any number of elements to fit a specific interior, removing the "it doesn't fit the space" objection.
6. **Elements in stock fast-track programme** — production in 15–20 working days instead of 40–60, at roughly 10% lower cost. Customisation: suspension length, cable colour, mounting, glass colour.
7. **Certified authenticity** — branded products with authenticity certification: an argument for clients buying an author's object rather than a lookalike fixture.
8. **Marketing support** — photography, 3D models, composition descriptions and renders for your presentations and project submissions.

### Dealer requirements

We build our network around partners who show the compositions in person. Photography does not convey scale or the way the light works — what sells is the display itself. Hence three mandatory conditions:

**1. Opening order from USD 50,000.**
The dealer places an order for any Vargov® Design compositions from the catalogue — chosen freely — to a value of no less than **USD 50,000** for their own showroom. We select the mix together, so that the display covers different price levels, categories and interior scenarios.

**2. Own display space (showroom).**
The partner must operate a showroom with floor area and ceiling height sufficient for a proper display of the ordered compositions — most pieces are suspended and only reveal themselves at the right height and with the right lighting. We take part in planning the layout, supplying 1:1 installation drawings and display recommendations.

**3. Selling from the display, with continuous replenishment.**
Compositions are sold to clients directly off the display — this is the dealer's key advantage: the client sees the piece in person and takes it away without waiting for production. Every sold piece is replaced by a new order, keeping the display continuously refreshed. The turnover benefits both sides: you get a live showroom and fast deals, we get a steady order flow and presence for new collections.

**Financial model.** Supply runs at factory prices with no brand markup. The dealer pays the factory directly: the order value plus the brand's remuneration of **10%**, as a single payment obligation. The dealer sets the end-customer price; the difference is the dealer's margin. Payment is 50% to start production and 50% before shipment.

The basis for the 10% is fixed in the agreement for each territory: in the Vietnam agreement it is the factory invoice amount; in the Kazakhstan agreement it is the Contract Value with the end customer (excluding installation, delivery and the dealer's services), with disclosure obligations and an audit right. State the basis explicitly when preparing an offer for a new partner.

Territory, exclusivity and the minimum annual volume are negotiated individually, based on region, business profile and volume.

### How an order works

1. **Order** — contact via WhatsApp/Telegram, cost and lead-time calculation, contract specification.
2. **Advance payment** — 50%.
3. **Production** — 40–60 working days: 3D model, installation drawing, manufacturing of elements. Under Elements in stock — 15–20 working days.
4. **Confirmation** — photo and video of the finished piece before shipping.
5. **Final payment** — the remaining 50%.
6. **Delivery** — 14–25 days depending on region.
7. **Installation** — optional; the service is available in Russia and the UAE.

**Packaging:** film, dense foam, branded cardboard inserts and tape, timber frame, plywood boxes, jute wrap and a final protective film layer. The 1:1 installation drawing is printed on banner fabric and packed inside one of the boxes; every cable is tagged with a number matching the drawing.
