## Description

A composition about foam and water. The matte tenderness of the opal rays and the cold shine of the chrome tail-spikes work as a pair: a soft body and a mirrored needle stitching the flight together.

The flock reassembles with every step the viewer takes — now a dense shoal, now sparse solitary silhouettes.

By day it is milky figures with a warm gradient inside and silver tails — a piece read through silhouette and soft transitions. In the evening the light inside the wings comes on evenly and calmly, without sharp highlights: the composition glows entire. The quietest of the brand's flying pieces.

## Where it works

Bedrooms, living rooms in a light register, spas, spaces beside water. Even light without glare is comfortable where people stay a while.

## Style

Marine modern and Scandinavian minimalism: flight without shine. The message is calm — a piece for an interior that does not raise its voice.

## Made to order

The number of figures, the density of the flock and the balance of white to chrome are all made to order.
