## Description

A meadow seen from below. The flowers face downward, each hanging at its own cable length, denser below and sparser above — an inverted pyramid the viewer looks into from underground.

The light hides in the gold hearts and runs down into the tassels, flaring at their tips as bright points. The white form gives no light at all — it works as silhouette, while all the brightness gathers in the hearts and threads.

By day it is a field of flowers hung from the ceiling — white, matte, calm. In the evening a luminous fringe of its own hangs beneath every flower: a swarm of fireflies, each with its own cup.

## Where it works

For generous spaces with high ceilings: atria, lobbies, double-height living rooms, winter gardens, spas.

## Style

Romantic modernism and eco-classicism: pale walls, living greenery, natural textures. The message is the first bloom — a composition about what pushes through before anything else and is valued for it.

## Made to order

The overall size, the number of flowers, the length of the tassels and the finish of the hearts are all made to order.
