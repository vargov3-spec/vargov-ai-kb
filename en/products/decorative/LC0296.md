## Description

A garland that does not end. The composition is built like a line of text: it runs the length of the table and has neither a beginning nor a full stop — only rhythm.

The rhythm is set by alternation: some leaves glow evenly and softly, others flare with sharp highlights. The light is hidden at the base of each and lifts it from below, leaving the upper side in shadow — foliage as seen by someone standing under the canopy.

By day it is a botanical garland, fine and graphic. In the evening it becomes a ribbon of light drawn across the room with every second leaf alight.

## Where it works

A linear format for very long tables, bar counters, galleries, banqueting halls, elongated meeting rooms.

## Style

Contemporary classic with a classical note: laurel, symmetry, noble proportions. The message is celebration — the composition reads as a wreath stretched above the gathering.

## Made to order

The length of the garland, the number of leaves and the balance of the two finishes are all made to order.
