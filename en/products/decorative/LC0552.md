## Description

A composition about a rising current. The petals are arrested in free flight, each with its own curve, turn and height; together they gather into a spatial whirlpool where rhythm, pause and depth are what matter.

This composition needs no light source to be bright: white enamel gives the purity, gold on the inner side of some elements gives warmth and weight.

By day the vortex lives on daylight: the white gloss reflects it softly, the gold reverses flare in the depth as precious accents, and the composition reads from below, from the side, from the stairs — a new frame every time. In the evening, under directed light, the gold catches fire more sharply while the white stays calm — the vortex acquires a rhythm of temperature.

## Where it works

Stairwells, double-height entrance halls, living rooms, reception rooms — wherever the composition is visible from several points. It requires no wiring but gains from directed light.

## Style

Contemporary sculpture and Art Deco: movement instead of ornament. The message is light and formal — a piece for an interior that wants volume without weight.

## Made to order

The scale of the vortex, the number of petals and the share of gold ones are all made to order.
