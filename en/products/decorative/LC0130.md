# Light composition LC0130

## Description

A composition about a moment. Every element is inclined the same way at the same angle — like grass laid down by wind. That single device turns the object into an event: the composition reads not as a thing but as the instant in which it was caught.

The line sags at the centre and rises at the ends — the arc of a crest. Above it runs a net of bare cables with small glowing points: spray above the wave, broken away from the mass.

By day it is a crest caught in a frame: white feathers mixed with bronze, the pure graphics of the inclination. In the evening the light grazes along each feather and emphasises the angle — the white ones glow evenly, the bronze give warm highlights, the points above scatter as sparks. A wave lit from within.

## Where it works

An elongated horizontal format — long tables, bar counters, meeting rooms, reception areas. The inclination sets a direction: the piece is oriented along the line of sight from the entrance.

## Style

Organic modernism and contemporary classicism: movement as the only decoration. The message is dynamic — a piece for a space that does not stand still.

## Made to order

The length, the arc pattern, the number of elements and the ratio of white to bronze are all made to order.
