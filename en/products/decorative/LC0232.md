## Description

Plasticity fixed at the moment the material was still yielding. Every element is twisted and rolled into a funnel, and the form is never repeated — this is not a series but a multitude of individual decisions.

The tinting is deep: on the curves the surface gives a mirrored gleam, against the light a warm amber. A single element reads as dense and transparent at once, depending on where the eye falls.

By day it is a cloud of crumpled gold foil — light, seemingly rustling. In the evening the light passes through several layers in a row, the source is lost, and the cloud becomes a swarm of burning petals with not a single visible lamp.

## Where it works

For living rooms with high ceilings, stairwells, lobbies, zones above a round table, banquet halls.

## Style

Art Deco and contemporary opulence: gilding, deep tones, large scale. The message is the generosity of the material — a composition about abundance presented without symmetry.

## Made to order

The overall diameter, the number of elements, the depth of the tinting and the drop height are all made to order.
