## Description

A composition about the passage from dawn to dusk. White and gold are not mixed here but drawn apart to the ends: the white boats gather on one side, the gold on the other, and in the middle they blend. The arc reads not as a pattern but as a timeline — a cold field warming toward gold.

The element is a narrow boat with high sharp prows and a deep hollow. The arc rises from one side, the elements lying in tiers and layers with a gradual gain in height.

By day the gradient reads calmly: white all but dissolves against a pale ground while gold holds the accent. In the evening the difference intensifies — the white diffuses the light into a soft even glow, the polished gold reflects it sharply and throws highlights. The transition becomes more legible than it is by daylight: the composition literally heats up toward one edge.

## Where it works

Generous rooms with irregular geometry — stairwells, curved halls, atria, double-height living rooms. The rise of the arc is designed for the specific volume.

## Style

Contemporary classicism with an Art Deco accent: white with gold, movement as the principal motif. The message is formal — a piece for a space where the first impression matters.

## Made to order

The length and rise of the arc, the number of elements and the point of transition from white to gold are all made to order.
