# Light composition LC0231

## Description

A snowfall with no lower boundary. The mass is dense at the centre and thins toward the edges, the top empty — only a net of cables. The object seems to hover rather than hang.

The shards are of varying calibre and scattered with no grid at all: from large plates down to crumbs. The light refracts on every facet and travels onward to the neighbours — the whole composition glows and the source is impossible to find.

By day it is snow stilled in the air — cold, silvery, almost soundless. In the evening rare warm points appear among the cold sparks, and the snowfall becomes a swarm of fireflies over water: a winter with summer loose inside it.

## Where it works

For large horizontal spaces: lobbies, dining rooms, meeting rooms, panoramic living rooms, banquet halls.

## Style

Nordic minimalism and contemporary classicism: pale walls, plenty of air, a subdued palette. The message is quiet — a composition about snow that falls while everyone sleeps.

## Made to order

The width and depth of the composition, the density of the shards and the share of warm points are all made to order.
