## Description

A composition about a migration over a northern sea. Birds of a smoky blue shade fly in a vertical stream — calmly and steadily, without drama: this is neither a flight nor a take-off but a long journey.

The muted blue-grey is a rare guest in light decor and precious for that reason: it ties the interior to the sky beyond the window. The gold breasts are the only warm accent.

By day the colour works at full strength: the smoky blue answers the sky, and the composition seems a continuation of the view. In the evening, under directed light, the matte surface gives deep shadows, the gold flares as points, and the stream becomes gathered and graphic.

## Where it works

Bedrooms, studies, staircases, interiors in a Scandinavian or Japanese aesthetic. The composition is decorative — it works without lamps but gains from side light.

## Style

Scandinavian minimalism and the Japanese school: the migration as a classic subject. The message is quiet — a piece for an interior that does not raise its voice.

## Made to order

The number of birds, the depth of the shade and the height of the stream are all made to order.
