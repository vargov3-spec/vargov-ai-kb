## Description

Marked Award Winning — the badge appears on the brand photography for this item.

Mass gives form, light gives movement. The whole idea rests on that division: the white bodies are motionless and silent, while the trail beneath them lives and stirs at the slightest movement of air.

The threads glow only at their tips, so a cloud of shimmering points hangs beneath every figure — the wake of particles behind a creature moving through water. The body does not glow at all: it only casts a shadow onto its own trail.

By day it is a flock of white sculptures below the ceiling — dense, matte, without a single highlight. In the evening the bodies recede into shadow and only the luminous rain remains: the flock disappears while its wake stays.

## Where it works

For large volumes: atria, lobbies, double-height living rooms, pools and spas, banquet halls.

## Style

Contemporary minimalism and biophilic design: white walls, water, natural textures, plenty of air. The message is the presence of the invisible — a composition about how movement is more visible than whatever is moving.

## Made to order

The number of figures, the length of the trails, the area and the height of the composition are all made to order.
