# Light composition LC0260

- Category: Decorative compositions
- Article code: LC0260

## Description

A score for three voices. The clear rings carry light along the arc and flare at the breaks, the gilded ones reflect and work as accents, the matte ones glow evenly — three behaviours of one beam in one composition.

The rings are open and hang in a vertical stream, catching at each other by eye but never touching. A gap always remains between them: the piece is about connection that requires no contact.

By day it is a scattering of bracelets laid out through the air. In the evening it becomes a stream of glowing arcs twisted into a spiral, where every arc burns in its own way.

## Where it works

A vertical format for stairwells, double-height spaces, bay windows, tall entrance halls.

## Style

Contemporary luxury and soft Art Nouveau: rounded forms, brass, pale walls. The message is consonance — three different characters gathered into one piece without argument.

## Made to order

The number of rings, the ratio between the executions and the height of the stream are all made to order.
