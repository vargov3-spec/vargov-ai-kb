## Description

A pod in motion, not a set of figures. The dolphins run in waves, some higher, some lower, all turned the same way — you read direction and speed.

The form is reduced to a pure silhouette, without detail: only the line of movement works. The light falls on the polished flanks and rolls out along the body — a glowing rib flares on every figure from nose to tail.

By day it is graphics against a dark ground — silhouettes, rhythm, pure line. In the evening not one source is visible directly: only the metal glows, and the pod moves through a body of water lit from the surface.

## Where it works

For generous volumes: atria, lobbies, pools and spas, panoramic living rooms, children's and family zones.

## Style

Marine modernism and contemporary minimalism: white walls, water, pale timber. The message is the joy of movement — a composition about animals that swim not out of need but because they can.

## Made to order

The length, the number of figures, the direction of the pod and the finish are all made to order.
