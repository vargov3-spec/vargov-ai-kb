## Description

A mosaic that does not hang down. The composition spreads beneath the ceiling as a flat layer — it does not occupy volume, it marks out a plane.

The pearlescence shifts from blue-violet into green, like a beetle's wing or a mussel shell: the colour is not fixed and changes with the angle of view. The light hides behind the elements and emerges at their edges, tracing every drop with a contour.

By day it is a scatter of pearls on dark velvet — dense, quiet, almost jewel-like. In the evening the contours light up and the mosaic becomes a star chart in which every pearl is ringed by its own orbit.

## Where it works

For ceilings of medium height where compactness matters: bedrooms, studies, lift lobbies, dressing rooms, corridors.

## Style

Soft Art Deco and contemporary classicism: pearlescence, mirrors, subdued tones. The message is preciousness in small compass — the composition needs no height and works where a chandelier is impossible.

## Made to order

The overall diameter, the number of drops and the tone of the pearlescence are all made to order.
