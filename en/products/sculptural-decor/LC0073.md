## Description

The same shell set upright. The layers no longer run in circles but converge upward toward a pointed apex, with the pearl shifted into the upper third: the form becomes teardrop-like, with a clear direction. The folds are larger and softer — a few broad waves instead of close accretions.

Direction changes the piece fundamentally. The round version is static; this one reaches upward.

By day the object reads as a mussel shell stood on end: polished gold with cool pearlescent reflections along the rims, pure sculpture. In the evening the light from the matte pearl runs down the folds and spreads across the full height — the lower part stays in shadow and works as form, the upper part burns. A drop of light in a metal setting.

## Where it works

Entrance halls, narrow corridors, bedrooms, stair flights, private reception rooms. The vertical format goes where there is no width: in piers and along flights.

## Style

Art Nouveau and organic modernism: a natural form with direction. The message is aspiring — a piece for a narrow space that needs height.

## Made to order

The dimensions, the depth of the relief, the metal finish and the position of the pearl are all made to order.
