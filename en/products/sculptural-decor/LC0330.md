## Description

The same system stripped of contrast — and denser in sound because of it. The white is removed and only a warm metallic tone remains, with deep shadows in the gaps.

The shade wanders from light to dark module by module, and that unevenness makes the surface alive, like old bronze that hands have touched. The gloss catches any source and scatters soft highlights across the wall.

The system can be built to any opening — from a full wall to a pier half a metre wide. This is not a product of fixed size but a language in which a border is constructed.

By day the shade wanders from light to dark module by module, and the surface lives like old bronze that hands have touched. In the evening the gloss catches any source and scatters soft highlights across the wall — the bronze lacework begins to shimmer.

## Where it works

A wide panel between zones or a narrow vertical section in a pier: a living room, an entrance hall, a restaurant, a boutique, a private study.

## Style

Art Deco and Eastern opulence: dark wood, deep colours, an abundance of metal. The message is restrained luxury — bronze lacework that works as a background rather than taking the solo.

## Made to order

The dimensions, the tone of the links and the configuration of the sections are all made to order.
