## Description

A composition about reflected space. Rounded mirrored boulders crumple like liquid metal and reflect an interior as a circular panorama — the piece does not show itself, it multiplies everything around it.

Light here is an accent rather than the main thing: gold points flare in the folds of every lens.

By day the composition lives exclusively on borrowed light: every lens gives its own distorted panorama of the room, and the drawing changes with every step. In the evening the gold points in the folds light up and the mirrored field acquires lights of its own — the reflection gets an anchor.

## Where it works

Accent walls and ceilings — entrance halls, living rooms, lobbies, reception desks, showrooms. The composition depends on its surroundings: it is placed where there is something to reflect.

## Style

Contemporary art and minimalism: an object with no image of its own. The message is conceptual — a piece for an interior that values an idea.

## Made to order

The number of lenses, their calibres and their layout across a surface are all made to order.
