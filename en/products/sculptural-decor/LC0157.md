## Description

A composition about light broken across facets. The triangular sails are slightly bowed, so a soft chiaroscuro without hard highlights appears across the plane — the screen becomes dimensional while staying pale and calm.

There are few gold triangles, set in free diagonal groups. The proportion is chosen so that gold reads as an accent rather than a pattern: the mass stays white, and the gold within it is an event.

By day the screen is nearly monochrome: the white faces catch daylight differently, and the whole difference rests on light and shade. In the evening a second life begins — the white diffuses the light into an even glow across the whole surface, while the gold sails intercept it and flare in hot patches. A calm wall turns out to be lit from within.

## Where it works

Bright interiors — zoning a living room, a screen in spas and pool areas, a partition in a reception room, a backdrop behind reception, a wall inset. Soft light without glare suits zones where people spend time.

## Style

Contemporary minimalism with a gold accent: a white field, pinpoint opulence. The message is light — a piece that brightens a space rather than weighing it down.
