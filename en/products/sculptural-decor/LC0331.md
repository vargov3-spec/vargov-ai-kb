## Description

A border the gaze passes through. The screen does not cut a space off, it separates it into layers: movement is visible behind it but not detail — exactly as much as is needed to keep the zones connected.

The modules are assembled into a rigid grid, but the surface of each is slightly convex and glossy, so the wall shimmers as you move along it. Alternating pale and dark links set a diagonal rhythm — a static structure reads as fabric.

By day the screen draws diamond shadows on the floor; in the evening, lit from the side, the gloss turns it into a wall of mother-of-pearl.

## Where it works

Zoning without walls: between a living room and a dining room, in an entrance hall, in a restaurant, in a showroom, beside a staircase, behind a reception desk.

## Style

Contemporary classic and softened Art Deco: the composition likes the company of velvet, brass and mirrors. The message is delicacy — dividing a space without cutting it apart.

## Made to order

The dimensions of the panel, the palette of the links and the density of the weave are all made to order.
