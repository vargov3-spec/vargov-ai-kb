## Description

A moon risen over a field. The sphere is set off centre, and around it the elements grow smaller and denser — as though drawn in by the gravity of a body heavier than any of them.

The main mass is matte white and rough; gold grains are scattered through it. The light emerges from beneath the sphere and spreads across the white field, emphasising the relief with a grazing beam.

By day it is a biological specimen under magnification — a colony, spores, something growing out of a plane. In the evening it is a moon over a plain: the same relief, read as a landscape rather than as a microcosm.

## Where it works

Feature walls in a hall, a bedroom, a private study, a meeting room, a gallery space.

## Style

Contemporary art and organic minimalism: white walls, an absence of ornament, sculptural furniture. The message is a change of scale — one and the same form reads through a microscope and through a telescope alike.

## Made to order

The diameter of the circle, the density of the elements and the share of gilding are all made to order.
