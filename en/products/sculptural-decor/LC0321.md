## Description

A rare format for the brand: not a luminaire but a piece of architecture. Here the light is built into a structure people run a hand along — and that changes the relationship with it.

The intent is to make an event of the passage rather than of a point. A staircase is usually what you walk through; here it is what you climb for.

By day the leaves work as stained glass: the cold blue reads against the light while the warm metal of the handrail keeps its own temperature. In the evening the light from the treads washes the whole flight in blue, and the contrast of the two lights carries the staircase entire.

## Where it works

Private residences, boutique hotels, showrooms, galleries — anywhere a staircase should be an event rather than a transit.

## Style

Contemporary luxury and Art Deco: marble, brass, deep colours, formal proportions. The message is ceremony — climbing a staircase like this becomes a staged gesture.

## Made to order

The length of the flight, the number of elements, the tone of the leaves and the metal finish are all made to order.
