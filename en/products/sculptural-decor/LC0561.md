## Description

A composition about a chase. Half the petals glow, half are faceted polished chrome, and around the circle they chase one another: light after shine, shine after light. The wall is set in motion.

The spiral is twisted so that the eye finds no beginning: the circle has no point of entry.

By day the object lives on sculptural plasticity: the chrome gathers the interior into shards of reflection while the pale petals hold the purity. In the evening a second life begins — a warm glow around the mirrored cores turns the composition into an interior jewel.

## Where it works

Accent walls — entrance halls, living rooms, reception rooms, reception desks. Objects like this do not fill a space, they shape it: they set a rhythm and gather attention.

## Style

Contemporary Art Deco and kinetic art: rotation as subject. The message is expressive — a piece that starts the conversation about an interior.

## Made to order

The diameter of the vortex, the number of petals and the balance of light to chrome are all made to order.
