## Description

A composition about a ring that burns only at its edge. The band is broad, its surface wavy, crushed and twisted along its whole length; the inner opening is not a circle but a living contour with swellings and lobes. There is no correct geometry anywhere.

A small matte sphere sits in the upper part, slightly off centre — and it is the only source. Its light runs along the inner rim, tracing the opening with a hot line, while the outer surface stays reflective.

By day it is a metal ring-sculpture: each wave carries its own elongated band of highlight, and as you walk past they shift — the object looks mobile. In the evening the outer part falls into shadow, and what remains is a hoop burning along its inner edge — the glowing contour of an emptiness.

## Where it works

Entrance halls, corridors, bedrooms, private living rooms, stair flights, reception areas. The light is directed into the ring and onto the wall — the fitting works as an accent rather than as illumination.

## Style

Contemporary sculpture and organic modernism: a ring without a single straight line. The message is spare — a piece for a wall that needs one object only.

## Made to order

The ring diameter, the width of the band, the metal finish and the calibre of the sphere are all made to order.
