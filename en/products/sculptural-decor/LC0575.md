## Description

A composition about a principle from which combinations grow. Every module is self-sufficient — one panel works as a wall light. But the real story begins in the assembly: the panels fall into a rhythm, with offsets and pauses, and as in origami one principle gives rise to endless variants.

Rectangular panels with a radial relief read as sheets of paper folded into a fan; at the centre of each is a gold mirrored window.

By day the relief reads through light and shade while the mirrored windows gather the room — the wall works as graphic art. In the evening warm light leaves the windows, the rays of the relief radiate from it, and every panel glows like a small sun behind a blind.

## Where it works

A strict horizontal above a console, a chequerboard order in a stairwell, a continuous light panel in a living room. The layout is chosen for a specific wall.

## Style

Art Deco and modular design: the panel as a unit of composition. The message is architectural — a piece that works with a wall rather than hanging on it.

## Made to order

The number of panels, the layout and the scale are all made to order.
