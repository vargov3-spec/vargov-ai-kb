# Light composition LC0255

- Category: Sculptural compositions & decor
- Article code: LC0255
- Product page: https://vargov.ru/en/catalog/lc0255

## Description

A horn with light pouring out of it. The form is taken from a wind instrument: a bell rolled from a sheet, its rim crumpled differently on every element.

The contrast decides everything: a cold blue back and a hot gold interior with a hammered texture. The light goes downward as a warm patch while the top stays blue and opaque — the object shows two temperatures at once.

By day it is a group of metal flowers — blue, dense, sculptural. In the evening it is a rank of horns pouring gold: sound you cannot hear but can see.

## Where it works

For long tables, bar counters, generous halls, restaurants, lobbies, meeting rooms.

## Style

Industrial luxury and contemporary modernism: dark walls, metal, large planes. The message is force and warmth — cold armour with fire hidden behind it.

## Made to order

The number of bells, the tone of the blue and the gilding finish are all made to order.
