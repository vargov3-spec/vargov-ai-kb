## Description

Shards, not blanks. The edges are torn, cut at random angles, the surface undulating — every plate reflects light unevenly, in bands, like foil smoothed out by hand.

One and the same set gives two characters: a free circle with gaps reads as an explosion, a regular grid as order. The composition changes its meaning with the layout while staying identical in content.

By day it is graphics made of highlights: the undulating surface catches the light of the room, and the pattern never repeats — it depends on what is happening around it. In the evening a contour lights up behind the plates and the wall becomes a scatter of luminous mirrors.

## Where it works

Feature walls, fireplace surrounds, stair flights, reception areas, private studies.

## Style

Contemporary art and brutalist minimalism: large planes, concrete, unworked stone. The message is the trace of destruction — a composition about the beauty of what was once whole.

## Made to order

The area of the layout, the number of plates, the layout scheme and the finish are all made to order.
