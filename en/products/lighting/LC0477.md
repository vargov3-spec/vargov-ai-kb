## Description

A composition about a stroke of the pen. Every curl is twisted its own way — as a hook, a loop, a comma: the gesture of a nib left in the air and set. Not one repeated line.

The composition joins baroque plasticity to contemporary minimalism: only form and the gold of the suspension points, no surplus detail.

By day it is a cluster of clear curls on gold capsules — a piece read through the plasticity of the bends and the highlights on the edges. In the evening light enters from above and runs along the curve: every curl glows along its edge, like a stroke made in luminous ink.

## Where it works

Living rooms, dining rooms, lobbies, reception rooms. The hanging cluster sets the lower boundary — clearance is needed beneath the composition.

## Style

Baroque retold in the language of minimalism: the curl without ornament. The message is expressive — a piece for an interior that needs a gesture.

## Made to order

The number of curls, their calibres and the shape of the cluster are all made to order.
