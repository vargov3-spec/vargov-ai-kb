## Description

A composition about kelp in a column of light. The ribbons wind around a vertical frame — they do not wrap it decoratively, they reach upward, as weed reaches for the surface. The vertical is full of movement: the ribbon seems to be still growing.

Sparks are scattered through the mass of the amber ribbons — the suspension you find in water lit from above.

By day it is a warm amber spiral on cold chrome: a contrast of two temperatures read through highlights. In the evening light travels down the ribbons, igniting the sparks in the mass and the gloss of the bends — the light descends into the depth as it does in real water.

## Where it works

Staircases, bay windows, tall wall piers, atriums. The vertical takes little floor area and works where there is no room for a bulky composition.

## Style

Marine Art Nouveau: the weed as a natural motif without stylisation. The message is warm and organic — a piece for an interior that avoids straight lines.

## Made to order

The height, the number of ribbons and the saturation of the amber tone are all made to order.
