## Description

A composition about a stone with a hole in it. The rings are thick, irregular, slightly skewed — as though turned by water rather than cast. There is not one true circle: the form belongs to the elements, not to a pair of compasses.

The rings are linked by gold pins with glowing ends and assembled into a dense field several layers deep. The mass is optically complex: through it you see the neighbouring rings, distorted as though through a lens.

By day it is a field of transparent sections — a piece read through distortions and overlays, the depth visible right through. In the evening light enters through the pins and spreads through the whole volume, and every ring glows along its inner contour. What remains is a net of glowing ovals hanging inside one another.

## Where it works

Equally good in two formats — a compact circle above a table or an elongated band along a counter. It reads both close up and from a distance, so it suits intimate rooms and large halls alike.

## Style

Natural minimalism and contemporary sculpture: the water-worn stone as a form. The message is calm and tactile — a piece for an interior that values substance.

## Made to order

The number of rings, the shape of the field and the density of the linking are all made to order.
