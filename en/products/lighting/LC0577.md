# Light composition LC0577

## Description

A composition about how a finish changes the voice of a form. The brand's familiar flagship curl is rendered in a dense glossy finish: the silhouette has become sharper, the form more composed, the plasticity more expressive. In the clear execution this element sounded light and airy; in the opaque white one it sounds substantial and sculptural. One character, two languages.

A fine gold capsule beside every element does not interfere with the form, it emphasises its rhythm.

By day the flock flies in a broad wedge, the gloss catching the reflections of its neighbours, and the composition reads as one movement — dense at the centre, thinned at the edges. In the evening the capsules draw warm verticals between the elements, and the wedge is stitched through with light.

## Where it works

Living rooms, lobbies, dining rooms, stairwells. The wedge has a direction: the composition is oriented along the route from the entrance.

## Style

Contemporary sculpture and Art Deco: the curl as a signature. The message is expressive — a piece for an interior that wants plasticity.

## Made to order

The number of elements, the spread of the flock and the ratio of mass to light are all made to order.
