# Light composition LC0326

## Description

A knot whose beginning cannot be found. The loops enter one another, cross, break off — a topology the eye tries to untangle and cannot.

The pink is muted almost to haze, and the ribbing adds a metallic cast.

The composition holds an international award.

By day the composition has to be walked around: each side reads as a new drawing and none of them gives the whole picture. In the evening the lights overlay each other where the ribbons cross, and bright knots appear there — a map the eye tries to travel.

## Where it works

It asks for space around it: the centre of a living room, an atrium, a generous lobby, a gallery hall, a showroom.

## Style

Contemporary art and gallery aesthetics: minimal furniture, maximum air, walls as a background. The message is an intellectual game — a piece that poses a question and does not answer it.

## Made to order

The dimensions of the tangle, the number of ribbons and the tone are all made to order.
