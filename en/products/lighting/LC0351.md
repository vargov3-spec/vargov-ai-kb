## Description

The same story in white. The absence of colour shifts the accent onto plasticity: you can see how each flower is coiled along a spiral, how the edge turns, how light flows out of the depth of the bowl. Nothing distracts from form.

Dark birds against a white ground read graphically, like silhouettes on paper: they give the composition rhythm and keep it from turning sugary. That one contrast carries the whole piece.

By day the object looks refined, expensive, faintly museum-like — white gloss and dark metal, nothing surplus. In the evening the flowers fill with warm light and the birds begin to reflect that glow, and the scene comes alive: the white stops being cold.

## Where it works

The most universal of the three versions — light interiors, bedrooms, living rooms in a neutral palette, stairwells. White works in any register, so the composition does not dictate a colour to the interior.

## Style

Art Nouveau and contemporary classic: white with a graphic accent. The message is restrained and expensive — a piece for an interior that chooses quiet.

## Made to order

The height of the cascade, the number of flowers and birds and the finish of the birds are all made to order.
