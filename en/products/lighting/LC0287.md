## Description

A form taken all the way to a sign. A body, two wings, not one detail — recognition rests on silhouette and tilt, and that turns out to be enough.

The gold is applied in points, only to the wingtips and the beaks: an accent within the drawing, not a finish. The light is hidden under each bird and lifts the wings from below, which leaves the edges half-transparent.

By day the flock reads as a sculptural group — white on white, pure plasticity. In the evening the birds glow from within and a wave of light travels the whole arc from end to end, the way movement travels through a real flock.

## Where it works

Large horizontal spaces: lobbies, conference halls, panoramic living rooms, banqueting halls, wedding venues.

## Style

Contemporary classic and minimalism: pale walls, simple forms, a minimum of colour. The message is clarity and peace — the composition reads as a symbol without turning into an allegory.

## Made to order

The number of birds, the span of the arc and the proportion of gilding are all made to order.
