## Description

Rain with neither a beginning nor an end. Dense gold at the top, clearer and clearer toward the bottom — the composition dissolves into the air instead of ending at an edge.

Inside every thread a chain of lights runs, and no thread repeats the rhythm of its neighbour. From a distance it is one continuous radiance; close up you see that it is assembled from thousands of separate sparks, each in its place.

The model holds an international award. The intent is simple and strong for that reason: the object does not depict rain, it occupies all the space that rain would occupy.

By day it is a gold gradient dissolving toward the bottom — a piece read through density and transition. In the evening a chain of lights runs inside every thread and no thread repeats the rhythm of its neighbour: continuous radiance from a distance, thousands of separate sparks up close, each in its place.

## Where it works

The format was made for large verticals: atriums, stair shafts, panoramic lobbies, double-height living rooms, banqueting halls.

## Style

Contemporary luxury and glamour: the composition was made to make an impression and works in interiors that are not embarrassed by that. The message is celebration — it turns any space into a formal one.

## Made to order

The height, the density of the threads and the gradient of the coating are all made to order.
