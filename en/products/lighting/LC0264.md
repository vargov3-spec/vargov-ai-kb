## Description

A shoal held still in the current. Every wing is curved in its own way: some folded almost in half, others barely bent — phases of a single movement laid out through the air.

The light spreads along the concave surface from inside: from below you see a glowing underside, from above the plate stays dark. The composition shows itself only to whoever stands beneath it — from above it barely exists.

By day it is a shoal mid-turn — smoky pink, iridescent, with inner currents running through the mass. In the evening it becomes a cloud of glowing wings at different heights with darkness falling away between them.

## Where it works

Generous halls with high ceilings: lobbies, double-height living rooms, restaurants, spas, pools.

## Style

Soft Art Nouveau and resort luxury: a pastel palette, pale fabrics, natural stone. The message is fluidity — the composition is about slow movement through a dense medium.

## Made to order

The number of elements, the spread of the shoal and the tone are all made to order.
