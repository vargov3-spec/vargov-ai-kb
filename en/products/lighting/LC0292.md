## Description

A composition made entirely of reflections. It has no colour of its own and almost no surface — only a mirror in which the interior, the light and the cluster itself converge.

Switched on, the object loses its form: the spheres turn into a gathering of bright patches and it becomes impossible to make out where one berry ends and the next begins. The piece disappears inside its own glow.

By day it is a jeweller's sculpture — precise, cold, every stem legible. In the evening it becomes a cluster of molten metal with neither boundaries nor detail.

The composition holds an international award.

## Where it works

A vertical format for stairwells, tall entrance halls, bay windows, double-height living rooms, showrooms.

## Style

High-tech and glamour at once: polished surfaces, glazing, no textiles. The message is shine without apology — the composition was made to impress and does it head-on.

## Made to order

The height of the cluster, the number of spheres and the finish are all made to order.
