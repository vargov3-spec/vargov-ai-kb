## Description

A composition about a shell that holds light. A broad bowl with a wavy rim and a deep dent at its centre: copper-rose and calm outside, mirrored within. One form, two opposite roles — restrained without, hot within.

The elements hang at varied levels, tilted at different angles, overlapping in places, but with enough air between them: the mass never congeals.

By day it is a cluster of copper forms — warm, quiet, working through soft reflections. In the evening a point lights up inside each shell: the light reflects off the mirrored layer, takes on copper and spreads across the whole inner surface, while the outer face stays rose and even. The mouths face different ways, and the composition looks different from every point.

## Where it works

Living rooms, dining rooms, lobbies, meeting rooms, zones above a long table. The varied tilts mean the piece is best assembled for a specific plan.

## Style

Warm Art Deco and contemporary classicism: copper and rose gold rather than yellow. The message is intimate and rich — a piece for a warm palette.

## Made to order

The overall size, the number of elements, the depth of the copper tone and the drop heights are all made to order.
