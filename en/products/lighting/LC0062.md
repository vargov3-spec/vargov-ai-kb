## Description

A composition about freezing rain. The rods are overgrown with small crystals along their whole length — uneven accretions, thicker in places, sparser in others. This is how a branch looks the morning after rain has frozen in flight: the crust builds not evenly but wherever it managed to.

The rods hang at varied angles — almost horizontal, oblique, crosswise — and intersect to form a three-dimensional lattice with no centre and no symmetry. A thicket of bare branches caught by frost.

By day it is an interlacing of branches — graphic, cold, living on the highlights along the crystals. In the evening every accretion lights its own point, and an uneven glowing line runs along each branch: where the crystals are denser, it is brighter. The polished ends flare as separate hard highlights. A thicket covered in luminous frost.

## Where it works

Generous rooms — lobbies, double-height living rooms, stairwells, winter gardens, restaurants. A lattice without a centre is built for the specific volume and has no principal face.

## Style

Nordic modernism and organic aesthetics: winter as motif. The message is cool and clean — a piece for an interior in a cold palette.

## Made to order

The overall size, the number of branches, the density of the crystals and the metal finish are all made to order.
