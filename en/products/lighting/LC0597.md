# Light composition LC0597

- Category: Lighting compositions
- Article code: LC0597
- Product page: https://vargov.ru/en/catalog/lc0597
- 3D model: https://3ddd.ru/users/vargov/models?tag=lc0597

## Description

A composition about the elements. A soft undulating lower edge makes the silhouette alive: at the bottom the composition does not break off, it runs down — banners in the wind, weed in the body of water, an aurora arrested in a room.

Plates of two characters, matte white and clear coloured, descend from the ceiling on fine cables; above them a scattering of miniature points holds the curtain in the air, like stars.

By day, as you move through the room, the layers reassemble and the drawing never repeats: in a cold register it is ice and turquoise, in a deep green one a dark field with a gold reverse. In the evening the points above light up, and the green version becomes an evening forest with the sun burning deep inside it, the cold one a radiance over water.

## Where it works

Lobbies, atriums, double-height living rooms, stairwells, restaurants. Related to the LC0598 series: the same plates, a different character — a city there, the elements here.

## Style

Contemporary sculptural installation and natural modern: the curtain as a form. The message is atmospheric — a piece that changes the state of a space.

## Made to order

The dimensions, the density, the palette and the drawing of the lower edge are all made to order.
