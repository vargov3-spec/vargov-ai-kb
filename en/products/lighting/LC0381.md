## Description

A composition about armour. The leaves hang in dense tiers, overlapping like plumage or like plates of armour, and the cone narrows downward: the form reads now as a folded wing, now as a fir cone, now as a suit of armour. The ambiguity works toward the image of protection — everything listed covers something of value.

Graphite-grey and matte outside, polished gold within. The most dramatic contrast in the brand's catalogue.

By day it is a dark sculptural mass — a blind, heavy piece that absorbs light. In the evening the light hidden under the feathers reflects off the gold reverse and only a warm radiance from the depth escapes: a dark exterior, a glowing interior. Armour with a fire under it.

## Where it works

Interiors with character — dark walls, wood, warm metal, subdued light. Against a pale ground the contrast stops working: the composition needs a dark context.

## Style

Dark luxe and brutal modernism: graphite with gold. The message is masculine and strong — a piece for an interior that does not flirt.

## Made to order

The number of feathers, the depth of the cone and the finish are all made to order.
