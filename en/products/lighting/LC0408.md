## Description

A composition about a wave of sails. The cones, opening downward, are gathered into a relief: a dense crest at the centre, sparse elements at the edges. The composition does not simply cover a space — it draws its shape, setting the height with light.

Two characters in one mass: matte white and honey amber, intermixed without order.

By day it is a wave of pale cones — a piece read through silhouette and the changes in height, calm and plastic. In the evening the white ones glow evenly, the amber ones fill with a dense sunset tone, and the wave acquires a relief of temperature on top of its relief of form: where there is more amber, it is warmer.

## Where it works

Lobbies, dining rooms, panoramic living rooms, restaurant halls. The cones are aimed downward — the light is soft and diffused, suitable for zones where people stay a while.

## Style

Scandinavian minimalism and mid-century: the cone as the purest of forms. The message is warm and calm — a piece for an interior that does not argue.

## Made to order

The number of cones, the balance of white to amber and the area of the wave are all made to order.
