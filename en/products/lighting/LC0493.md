# Light composition LC0493

## Description

A composition about a stamen made of pure light. A gold needle with a glowing filament passes through every bud — the light burns along the whole axis, exactly where a real flower has its stamen. Structure and botany have coincided.

The cluster is dense, matte white intermixed with clear amber; needle pins above the buds stitch the composition with a gold rhythm.

By day it is a cluster of buds running from cream to honey — a piece read through the form of the drop and the transition of tones. In the evening the white ones give a matte, calm glow and the amber ones a deep, warm one, and the cluster shifts along its whole height while the gold axes show through it as vertical threads.

## Where it works

Living rooms, dining rooms, stairwells, reception rooms. The dense cluster is built downward: clearance is needed beneath the composition.

## Style

Art Nouveau and jewelled Art Nouveau: a flower with a metal core. The message is warm and expensive — a piece for an interior in a gold register.

## Made to order

The number of buds, the balance of white to amber and the shape of the cluster are all made to order.
