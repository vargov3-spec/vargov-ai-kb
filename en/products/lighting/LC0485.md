## Description

A composition about water in a frame. A strict contour and free plasticity within — a formula that makes the object architectural and alive at once: the frame holds, the wave moves.

Clear ribbons with a bubbled mass roll inside the frame, and glowing strips light every crest.

By day, from below, the composition reads as the surface of water raised above the table: the bubbles are visible against the light, the crests catch light from the window. In the evening light lifts every crest from within, and the water begins to move before your eyes — the frame stays still while the contents live.

## Where it works

Above a long table, a kitchen island, a reception desk. The linear format is calculated for the length of the zone; the light is directed downward and gives working illumination.

## Style

Contemporary minimalism and architectural design: the frame as a device. The message is rational and beautiful at once — a piece for an interior that loves order.

## Made to order

The length, the number of waves and the finish of the frame are all made to order.
