## Description

Marked Award Winning — the badge appears on the brand photography for this item.

Pure plasticity of line. Two bands run one above the other, rising, falling, twisting at their ends and parting — not one repeated passage along the whole length.

The ribbing changes density as it turns: the ribbon almost disappears, then fills with colour. The lights along the rim are set unevenly and emphasise the curves precisely where the line turns.

By day it is two smoky bands frozen in the air — nearly graphics, nearly a technical drawing. In the evening the light runs along the rim and scatters across the ribbing in fine highlights: what remains is a trace left by light in the dark — a stroke with no visible hand behind it.

## Where it works

An elongated horizontal format for long spaces: dining halls, meeting rooms, lobbies, reception areas, galleries.

## Style

Contemporary minimalism and gallery aesthetics: empty walls, large planes, an absence of small decoration. The message is the gesture — a composition about the movement of a hand rather than about an object.

## Made to order

The length of the bands, the wave pattern, the tone and the number of lights along the rim are all made to order.
