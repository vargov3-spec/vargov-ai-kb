## Description

A collection of vessels that never held anything. Bellied flasks, drawn-out decanters, squat retorts — the forms are taken from craft and released from function.

Light pours into each cavity from below, like a liquid, and stops at the neck. The vessel turns out to be full — not of a substance but of a glow, and that substitution is what the whole thing was conceived for.

No form repeats another: the asymmetry here is a signature rather than carelessness.

By day it is a workshop shelf lifted into the air: the forms read as silhouettes and no two are alike. In the evening light pours into each cavity from below, like a liquid, and stops at the neck — the vessels turn out full, not of a substance but of a glow.

## Where it works

The cascade narrows downward — a format for stairwells, bay windows, double-height living rooms, wine rooms and restaurant halls.

## Style

Eclecticism and artisan luxury: exposed brick, wood, antiques mixed with the contemporary. The message is collectorly — a piece for someone who gathers objects with a history and values the trace of a hand.

## Made to order

The number of vessels, their forms and the depth of the cascade are all made to order.
