## Description

A composition about a storm. Fan-shaped wings twist through space in swift spirals — the feathers of a firebird, or petals caught by the wind. The movement is taken to the limit: this is one of the brand's most temperamental pieces.

The colour works at the same limit: a deep ruby with not one softening shade.

By day it is red vortices with chrome cores — a dense colour read through the twist and the rod texture of the fans. In the evening capsules within ignite the fans with a deep garnet radiance and the space around is charged: the light does not illuminate, it heats.

## Where it works

Interiors that need character — restaurants, bars, art spaces, living rooms with a bold palette. Saturated red takes command of a room: neutral surfaces are needed nearby.

## Style

Contemporary sculptural installation and theatrical aesthetics: colour and movement as a statement. The message is passionate — a piece for a space that is not afraid to be loud.

## Made to order

The spread of the vortex, the number of fans and the saturation of the red are all made to order.
