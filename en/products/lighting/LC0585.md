## Description

A composition about a moment caught in a fold. The plasticity of folded cloth is arrested — every element is a small sculpture in which the folds catch light and shadow.

This is a modular system rather than a chandelier in the usual sense: the elements assemble into a ring, a cascade, an elongated line, a dense volume or an almost airy structure. The composition changes with the angle of view and holds its character every time.

By day the white reads through the plasticity of the folds, the dark elements add contrast and depth, and the gold centre turns every module into a point of attraction in its own right. In the evening the light does not flood the form — it comes out from within, like breathing: a soft accent that emphasises the relief and makes the white warm and the dark velvet.

## Where it works

Living rooms, lobbies, dining rooms, stairwells, reception rooms. The modularity allows the composition to be assembled to any geometry — from a compact ring to an architectural volume.

## Style

Contemporary sculpture and Art Deco: the fold as a module. The message is flexible and expressive — a piece that is built to the space.

## Made to order

The size, the shape, the number of elements and the balance of light to dark are all made to order.
