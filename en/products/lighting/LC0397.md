## Description

A composition about a dragonfly's wing. The finest longitudinal hatching on every leaf is not decor but structure: on a real wing these are the veins that hold the transparent film. The author uses that literally — in the evening the light travels along exactly that hatching.

The branches interlace, the feathers point in different directions, the density grows toward the centre: the composition reads as a thicket rather than a bouquet.

By day, from a distance, it is a pink cloud; up close a collection of separate feathers, each at its own angle, lilac-pink intermixed with smoky and clear. In the evening light from the joints lifts the feathers from below, the hatching flares, and every leaf comes to resemble a dragonfly's wing.

## Where it works

Living rooms, boudoirs, bedrooms, reception areas, boutiques. The dusty pink register is rare in light objects and needs neutral surroundings so as not to argue with the finishes.

## Style

Art Nouveau and romantic modern: the insect and the plant as classic motifs. The message is feminine and expensive at once — a piece for an interior with fine taste.

## Made to order

The length of the vine, the palette and the density of the thicket are all made to order.
