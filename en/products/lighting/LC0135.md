## Description

A composition about a root. A long winding tube with smooth turns and swellings — a form with no geometry: neither a root nor a stem nor a weed ever has any. The author refuses repetition, and every element is bent its own way.

The mass descends from a round mirrored base and narrows below. The stems interweave and touch here and there: the thicket reads as continuous, yet every individual stem is visible.

By day it is a mass of white coral suspended below the ceiling — dense, calm, monochrome. In the evening the light enters a stem at its end and travels its whole length, and the milky surface diffuses it so that not a point but the entire stem glows, evenly and softly. A thicket lit underwater.

## Where it works

Living rooms with high ceilings, lobbies, spas, stairwells. The composition builds downward, so what matters is not floor area but clear height beneath it.

## Style

Organic modernism and marine aesthetics: a line without a single angle. The message is soft and enveloping — a piece for an interior where people rest.

## Made to order

The base diameter, the number of stems, the element lengths and the tone of the glow are all made to order.
