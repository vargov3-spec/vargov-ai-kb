## Description

Stones split by hand rather than worked on a machine. The faceting is deliberately irregular — the piece is about chance being more precise than symmetry.

Inside each element its own lamp burns, and the facets work as prisms: the light comes out in bundles, breaks up, reflects in its neighbours. Varying the drop height gives the scattering volume — a cloud of jewels suspended in the air.

By day it is a collection of minerals laid out on invisible shelves. In the evening it becomes the heat of coals lifted to the ceiling and left there to burn down.

## Where it works

A flexible format: a compact group above a table or a wide field in a hall, a lobby, a restaurant, a private living room.

## Style

Contemporary luxury and brutal opulence: rough stone, dark metal, leather. The message is possession — the composition speaks of value directly, without circumlocution.
