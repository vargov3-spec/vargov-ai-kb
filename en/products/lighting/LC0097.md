## Description

A composition about an armful. The stems spread from a round base outward and downward, each bending its own way: not a bouquet set in a vase but an armful thrown open in every direction. Between them runs a dense fringe of straight rods — what remains of the stems that have no flower.

The cups with their ragged wavy rims are open like a poppy, or like a flower that has just unfurled. The tone is pomegranate-crimson, almost opaque in places, lightening to pink in others.

By day the composition reads as a bouquet — saturated colour on gold, dense and painterly. In the evening the light passes through the body of the colour and the petals fill from within: the tone becomes deeper and hotter than by daylight, while the stems pick up the reflection and glow as fine lines. An armful of embers on gold legs.

## Where it works

Living rooms, dining rooms, reception rooms, restaurant floors, boudoirs. The saturated tone needs a calm backdrop: against a coloured wall the flower is lost.

## Style

Art Nouveau and romantic classicism: the flower as the principal subject of an interior. The message is passionate and warm — a piece for a space with character.

## Made to order

The base diameter, the number of flowers, the stem lengths and the depth of the pomegranate tone are all made to order.
