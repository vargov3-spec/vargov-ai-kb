# Lighting composition LC0545-1

## Description

A composition about a garden on water. Glass lily pads — green and clear, with a radial relief running from the centre to the wavy edge — hang on threads in tiers, each turned at its own angle, like a leaf on water stirred by the wind.

Brass stems pass between the pads: slender, curved, flared at the tip. Some reach upward, others hang below the cloud — the stems set the rhythm and read as graphic lines over the glass.

By day it is green and clear on warm brass: the relief catches the light of the room, the green glass deepens, the clear glass glints. In the evening light comes on at the centre of the clear pads and at the tips of the stems — points of fire inside the cloud, with the relief spreading from them in rays.

## Where it works

Sitting rooms, dining rooms, lobbies, stairwells. The cloud is assembled as a wide patch over the zone or as a tall cascade.

## Style

Art Nouveau and the natural theme: a garden on water as the subject. The message is calm and warm — green glass beside brass.

## Made to order

The number of pads, the ratio of green to clear glass and the shape of the cloud are all made to order.
