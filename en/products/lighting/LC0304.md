## Description

Light hidden inside something that absorbs light. The surface is matte, downy, almost textile — it kills highlights completely, and that is the whole calculation.

Hundreds of points scattered through the mass read against such a background with full contrast, like sparks deep in moss. The composition does not shine — it smoulders, and that is a rare state for a light object.

By day it is a warm patch of colour with a rich texture, a piece you sense by touch rather than by glow. In the evening the mass disappears and what remains is a constellation suspended in the air with nothing holding it.

## Where it works

Generous interiors with high ceilings: lobbies, lounges, spas, private clubs, living rooms with a fireplace.

## Style

Eco-luxury and comfortable minimalism: natural fabrics, wood, a muted palette. The message is warmth and shelter — the composition reads as something alive and soft that asks to be touched.

## Made to order

The diameter of the dome, the density of the pile, the tone and the number of light points are all made to order.
