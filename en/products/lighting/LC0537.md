# Light composition LC0537

## Description

A composition about a trace on ice. The ribbons wind in smooth spirals — the crests of waves, untied bows, the arcs left by a skater: a form with no beginning and no end, which the eye follows without stopping.

The rods stay almost colourless: all the expression is in movement and light, not a gram of decor.

By day it is a clear streamer in the air — a piece read through trajectory and with almost no mass. In the evening gold capsules woven into the ribbons ignite them from within at warm intervals: the light comes not as an unbroken line but as a dotted one — like a trace that shows and then does not.

## Where it works

Large spaces — atriums, double-height living rooms, lobbies where the streamer has somewhere to fly. The trajectory is designed for a specific volume.

## Style

Contemporary sculptural installation: the line as the only material. The message is light and dynamic — a piece for a space that wants movement.

## Made to order

The length and trajectory of the ribbons and the number of coils and points of light are all made to order.
