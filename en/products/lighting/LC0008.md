## Description

A composition about a thicket in bud. Inside — a dense stand of fine clear rods, a bristling hatch; outward and downward run curved stems, each ending in a large matte drop. Hard within, soft without: the structure of almost any plant.

The stems carry the drops well beyond the main volume, and a fringe forms around the edges. Inside the stand the drops hang closer and lower.

By day it is a thicket in bloom: clear hatching and white spheres on curved arcs, read through silhouette. In the evening the light lives in two places — the rods carry it along their length and flare at the ends as points, while the drops glow from within in an even milky tone, without flare. A bristling hatch inside and soft glowing spheres around the edge: a swarm of fireflies around a clear column.

## Where it works

Living rooms with high ceilings, stairwells, lobbies, atria. The fringe extends beyond the main envelope: allow clearance to the sides.

## Style

Art Nouveau and organic modernism: a plant with a different character inside and out. The message is alive — a piece for an interior that loves natural forms.

## Made to order

The overall size, the number of stems, the calibre of the drops and the drop heights are all made to order.
