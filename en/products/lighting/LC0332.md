## Description

Ice from a river shallow, set on edge. The surface is rough, chipped, full of inclusions — the substance is deliberately left imperfect, because perfect ice does not exist.

The light enters from above and gets caught in the mass rather than passing through. The plate glows not against the light but from within, diffusely, like a real floe under a low sun.

The ring form closes the composition on itself: it has no front and no back, it is equally composed from any side. A crown with every point alight.

By day it is ice from the shallows: a rough chipped surface with inclusions, a piece read through texture and irregularity. In the evening the light enters from above and gets caught in the mass rather than passing through — the plate glows from within, diffusely, like a real floe under a low sun.

## Where it works

The ring form is made for round tables, rotundas, the zone above a kitchen island, private dining rooms.

## Style

Nordic minimalism and eco-modern: pale wood, linen, stone, a muted palette. The message is natural honesty — the composition does not pretend to be precious, it pretends to have been found.

## Made to order

The diameter of the ring, the number of plates and the height of the candles are all made to order.
