# Light composition LC0335

- Category: Lighting compositions
- Article code: LC0335
- Product page: https://vargov.ru/en/catalog/lc0335

## Description

A polar sky carried into a room. The colour moves from deep emerald at the top to clear at the bottom — the way a real aurora melts toward the horizon, having no lower edge at all.

The composition puts itself out. By day it is a dense green-and-blue curtain; in the evening the mass disappears and only thousands of sparks remain, hanging in layers at different depths. An object that loses its body in the evening and leaves the light behind.

The model holds an international award. The intent lies in refusing an edge: the composition does not end, it dissolves.

## Where it works

A format for large verticals — atriums, stair shafts, panoramic lobbies, double-height living rooms, wherever the height can be shown in full.

## Style

Contemporary luxury and Nordic Art Nouveau: a cold palette, panoramic glazing, dark noble surfaces. The message is contemplative — the composition gives not light but the state of a northern night.

## Made to order

The height, the density of the threads and the depth of the gradient are all made to order.
