# Light composition LC0489

- Category: Lighting compositions
- Article code: LC0489
- Product page: https://vargov.ru/en/catalog/lc0489
- 3D model: https://3dsky.org/3dmodels/show/svetovaia_kompozitsiia_vargov_r_design_lc0489_1

## Description

A composition about a current stopped mid-movement. Every ribbon has a plasticity of its own: here a wave, there stretched cloth, there a jet of air. There are no two alike, because a current does not repeat itself.

In the mass there are hundreds of air bubbles; gold clasps catch the ribbon along its whole length. The fine suspensions dissolve into the dark, and the columns hover.

By day it is a clear forest of verticals: the bubbles read against the light, the ribbons as smooth waves, a piece light and almost invisible. In the evening light enters through the clasps and ignites the bubbles — the mass fills with sparks and the columns become luminous right through.

## Where it works

Staircases, double-height living rooms, window displays, atriums. The forest of verticals is designed for a specific height and reads from several levels.

## Style

Contemporary sculptural installation: the movement of water as a motif. The message is light and fluid — a piece for an interior that avoids stasis.

## Made to order

The number of ribbons, their heights and the density of the forest are all made to order.
