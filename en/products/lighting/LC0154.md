# Light composition LC0154

## Description

A composition about a hidden core. An elongated lozenge is twisted on its long axis, and the twist creates pockets — inner folds you cannot see into from outside. It is these that glow, not the elements themselves: the composition is built so that the source always hides in the fold.

The pyramid is dense at the centre and thinned toward the edges, with single lozenges descending below. All are turned at random, and the mass reads as volumetric, with hollows and gatherings.

By day it is a swarm of twisted ribbons — white with gold, a form without light, living on plasticity alone. In the evening the outer surfaces stay opaque while the inner folds fill with light: what burns is not the mass but its core. A cloud with fire hidden inside it.

## Where it works

Living rooms with high ceilings, stairwells, lobbies, banquet halls. The pyramidal build needs height: pressed to the ceiling, it loses its silhouette.

## Style

Contemporary Art Deco: the twisted ribbon as the principal motif, white with gold as the palette. The message is ceremonial — a piece for a formal space.

## Made to order

The overall size, the number of elements and the ratio of white to gold are all made to order.
