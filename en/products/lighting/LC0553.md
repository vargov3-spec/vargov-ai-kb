# Light composition LC0553

## Description

A composition about a balance of opposites. The white glossy boat-shapes open with a soft glow from within; the chrome ones work by pure reflection, breaking an interior into highlights. Softness and cold shine gathered into one form.

The cloud is dense and low; light passes between the surfaces and is reflected back and forth in the facets.

By day the chrome elements gather the room while the white ones hold a quiet background: the cloud seems to reassemble slowly as you walk past. In the evening the white ones light from within while the chrome ones go on only reflecting — and the piece separates for good into two parts sounding together.

## Where it works

Living rooms, dining rooms, meeting rooms, lobbies. The low cloud requires no great height and suits medium ceilings.

## Style

Contemporary minimalism and Art Deco: two behaviours of light as a device. The message is restrained — a piece for an interior that loves a nuance.

## Made to order

The size and density of the cloud and the ratio of white to chrome are all made to order.
