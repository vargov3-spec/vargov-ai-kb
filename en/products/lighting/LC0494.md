## Description

A composition about a leaf fall twisted by wind. The funnel narrows downward, the elements circling densely — the form of a vortex that has drawn fallen leaves into it.

The light hides in the gold: points of warm radiance are scattered through the vortex like sparks in a fire of leaves. Clear green among the whites works as a fresh accent.

By day it is a white funnel with gold tips and occasional green — a piece read through its twist and silhouettes, light and mobile. In the evening the points light up inside the vortex and it begins to turn before your eyes: the light runs down the spiral from the top.

## Where it works

Stairwells and double-height halls. The funnel form requires height — the composition is built downward and reads from several levels.

## Style

Natural Art Nouveau and contemporary sculpture: the vortex as a form of movement. The message is alive and spring-like — a piece for a space with character.

## Made to order

The number of petals, the share of green and the height of the vortex are all made to order.
