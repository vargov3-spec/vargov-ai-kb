## Description

A surface that flows into itself. The form folds and unfolds without a single corner — a bend, an overlap, a wave at the moment it turns over: movement is recognised, not an object.

Every element reflects light at its own angle, so moving along the composition sends waves of highlights running across it. The object does not move, yet it never looks frozen.

By day it is a dense dark mass — cold, with a blue cast. In the evening the light sets the elements alight from within, and the mass breaks up into separate sparks.

The composition holds an international award.

## Where it works

Large spaces: atriums, lobbies, panoramic halls, pools, spa areas, showrooms.

## Style

Contemporary luxury and biomimicry: large volumes, smooth surfaces, a dark palette. The message is natural mechanics — beauty derived from the way living things are built.
