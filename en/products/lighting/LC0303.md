## Description

A surface that flows into itself. The form folds and unfolds without a single corner — the tail of a fish, the wing of an insect, a wave at the moment it turns over: everything is recognised at once and nothing separately.

Every scale reflects light at its own angle, so moving along the composition sends waves of highlights running across it. The object does not move, yet it never looks frozen.

By day it is armour — dense, cold, blue-steel in cast, like blued metal. In the evening the light sets the scales alight from within, and the armour turns into a fish heading for deep water.

The composition holds an international award.

## Where it works

Large spaces: atriums, lobbies, panoramic halls, pools, spa areas, showrooms.

## Style

Contemporary luxury and biomimicry: large volumes, smooth surfaces, a dark palette. The message is natural mechanics — beauty derived from the way living things are built.

## Made to order

The dimensions, the number of scales and the tone are all made to order.
