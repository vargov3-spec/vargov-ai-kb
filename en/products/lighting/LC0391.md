## Description

A composition about opening. Some flowers are fully open, some are only beginning — the cascade shows the process rather than the result. The spiral twists, the flowers look in different directions, and the stream reads as growing upward or descending depending on where you stand.

The pink here is powdery, almost flesh-toned: it makes the composition warm and alive rather than decorative.

By day it is a vertical stream of soft pink on gold stems — a piece read through colour and the plasticity of the turned-back petals. In the evening light inside every flower shows through the fine petals and makes them glow from inside: the cascade fills from within, like skin against the light.

## Where it works

A vertical format — stairwells, double-height living rooms, tall niches, bay windows. The spiral reads as you climb and as you walk around it.

## Style

Art Nouveau and Eastern classicism: the lotus as a symbol of opening. The message is warm and feminine — a piece for an interior that loves softness.

## Made to order

The height of the cascade, the number of flowers and the depth of the pink tone are all made to order.
