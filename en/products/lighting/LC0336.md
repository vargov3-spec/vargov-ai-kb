## Description

A form impossible to repeat twice. The lines do not follow the rules of a sphere, they fall as thread falls — freely, overlapping, with hollows and thickenings.

This is a composition about the hand-made: behind the apparent chaos stands exact calculation, but the eye reads not calculation but a nest, a cocoon, a ball of yarn — something wound rather than drawn.

The light is born at the knots of the weave and scatters in hundreds of points. The honey tone takes away the cold, and the sphere begins to seem warm to the touch — an illusion the whole object rests on.

By day it is a tangle of overlapping lines with hollows and thickenings: behind the apparent chaos stands calculation, but the eye reads a nest rather than a drawing. In the evening the light is born at the knots of the weave and scatters in hundreds of points, the honey tone takes away the cold — the sphere begins to seem warm to the touch.

## Where it works

A spherical form has no front: it works in the centre of a space, above a round table, in a double-height living room, in an entrance hall, in a restaurant.

## Style

Eco-modern and comfortable minimalism: wood, linen, rattan, warm walls. The message is domestic warmth — honey light and the form of a nest read as a promise of comfort.

## Made to order

The diameter of the sphere, the density of the weave and the tone of the threads are all made to order.
