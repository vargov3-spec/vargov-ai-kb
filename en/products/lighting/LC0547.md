## Description

A composition about a reef raised from the sea floor. Two kinds of element form a colony: openwork blue-violet nets, like the fans of gorgonians, and white textured cones with a cellular surface, like colonies of polyps. Between them, cold points of light — plankton.

The composition is built on the contrast of dense against clear: the nets let the gaze pass right through, the cones hold the volume. From every angle the reef reassembles, opening new gaps and shadows.

By day it is a marine palette — ultramarine, lilac, white: a piece fresh and deep at once, read through openwork and texture. In the evening the points between the elements shimmer as suspension, the nets show through and the cones stay dark — the colony turns out to be at depth.

## Where it works

Spas, pools, bathrooms, living rooms in a marine register, children's rooms. The openwork structure steals no light and suits shaded zones.

## Style

Marine modern and bio-design: the reef as a form with no author. The message is natural — a piece for an interior beside water.

## Made to order

The composition of the reef, the balance of the colours and the size of the colony are all made to order.
