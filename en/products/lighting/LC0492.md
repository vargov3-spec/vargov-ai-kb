## Description

A composition about a vine on a suspended frame. The frame is twisted into a spiral, the branches spread out from it, and the vine flowers in mid-air — a gardener's device: a plant is trained up a support.

Along the branches grow glossy domes ranging from the size of a palm to the size of a saucer, and between them clusters of clear bubbles and curls.

By day it is a painterly volume in a powdery pink register — tender and rare for light decor; the composition reads through the silhouette of the vine and the calibres of the domes. In the evening a warm light comes on inside the domes, the bubbles pick it up and scatter it in sparks, and the vine begins to flower in light.

## Where it works

Entrance halls, living rooms, boudoirs, bedrooms, boutiques. The vine on its frame is hung from the ceiling, including in a stairwell.

## Style

Art Nouveau and romantic modern: the vine as a classic motif. The message is feminine and tender — a piece for an interior with a soft palette.

## Made to order

The height of the vine, the number of domes and the density of the scattering are all made to order.
