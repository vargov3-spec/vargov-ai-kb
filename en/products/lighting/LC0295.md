## Description

A form that is barely there. The elements are so clear that what you see is not them but the pattern of scoring, hanging in the air.

The scoring works as a diffraction grating: light breaks on the ridges into rainbow highlights, and the feather begins to shift colour without a single drop of pigment in it. The rainbow here is not applied — it is computed.

By day the composition reads as a collection of transparent feathers laid out through the air. In the evening point sources ignite the scoring and the group shimmers the way a slick of oil shimmers on water.

## Where it works

Generous interiors with a quiet background — pale walls, minimal decor around: living rooms, dining rooms, galleries, reception rooms.

## Style

Minimalism and optical design: clean planes, no ornament, precise light. The message is physics instead of decor — beauty obtained from the properties of a substance rather than from a pattern applied to it.

## Made to order

The number of feathers, their calibres and the density of the group are all made to order.
