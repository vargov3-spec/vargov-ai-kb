## Description

A composition about a molecule. Amber bubbles join polished spheres into clusters — bonds, chains, points of attraction: an enlarged fragment of matter, alive and a little chaotic.

The piece has both scientific precision and artistic freedom: the elements look random and assemble into a considered formula.

By day it is warm volumes with cold chrome nodes — a piece read through structure and reflections. In the evening light comes on inside the amber volumes, the mirrored inner surface turns every bubble into a small furnace with warm reflections, and the chrome between them stays cold: the molecule breathes on contrast.

## Where it works

Living rooms, lobbies, meeting rooms, showrooms, lofts. The clusters spread through a volume — the composition is designed for a specific space.

## Style

Scientific aesthetics and mid-century: the structure of matter as a form. The message is intellectual — a piece for an interior with an idea.

## Made to order

The number of clusters, their make-up and their spread through the space are all made to order.
