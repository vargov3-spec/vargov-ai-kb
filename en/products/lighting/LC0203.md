## Description

A meadow with fewer flowers in it than it seems. There are not many buds, but hundreds of small beads between them — the density is created not by the flowers but by what has not yet bloomed.

The light is distributed in patches: some hearts burn, others stay dark. That is how a real meadow looks, with the sun breaking through unevenly — evenness here would be a falsehood.

By day it is a botanical study — fine, pale, almost weightless. In the evening the meadow rises to the ceiling and lights up from within: the flowers do not all come on at once, and the field begins to shimmer in patches.

## Where it works

For living rooms with high ceilings, stairwells, lobbies, winter gardens, wedding venues.

## Style

Romantic modernism and eco-classicism: pale walls, living greenery, natural fabrics. The message is the first bloom — a composition about early spring, when not everything is flowering yet.

## Made to order

The overall diameter, the number of flowers, the ratio of pearlescent to gold and the density of the buds are all made to order.
