## Description

Metal caught in the act of flowing. The form has run and crumpled — it looks not made but happened, as though it had been stopped a second before it flowed further.

Some drops are opaque and mirrored, others glow from within. Because of that the cluster burns in every other element: the light here is selective rather than general, and the composition reads as a branch on which half the berries have ripened.

By day it is a gathering of mercury bubbles with a cool blue-steel shift. In the evening gold kindles inside them — a warm patch amid cold metal, like fire seen through ice.

## Where it works

A horizontal format for long tables, generous living rooms, lobbies, restaurants, private studies.

## Style

High-tech and contemporary opulence: polished surfaces, transparency, a dark palette. The message is a controlled element — a composition about a material that was once liquid and remembers it.

## Made to order

The number of drops, the ratio of mirrored to luminous and the span of the branches are all made to order.
