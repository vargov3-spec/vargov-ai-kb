## Description

A leaf that changes colour as it turns. One side is cool and matte, the other warm and mirrored — the element switches over entirely at the slightest swing.

The composition hangs at random, some leaves dropping below the main mass. The upper zone stays transparent — only the net of cables — so the object looks as if it were hovering, for all its size.

By day it is a leaf-fall stilled in the air: silver and gold intermixed, not one identical inclination. In the evening the light scatters unevenly, reflecting now cool, now warm, and the composition shimmers the moment you shift a step: a swarm of sparks that lives off the viewer's movement.

## Where it works

An elongated horizontal format — for long dining tables, bar counters, lobbies, meeting rooms, restaurant floors.

## Style

Contemporary classicism and soft Art Deco: mirrors, brass, pale walls. The message is variability — a composition about a thing that never shows the same face twice.

## Made to order

The length, the number of leaves, the ratio of cool to warm and the drop heights are all made to order.
