## Description

A composition about an enfilade. Every element works as an arch through which the next one is visible: the composition is built on depth and rhythm rather than on density. The gaze travels inward, as through a run of openings in an old city.

Warm vertical capsules strengthen the architectural association — windows lit at sunset.

By day it is a clear enfilade: the upward-reaching silhouettes read through overlay and depth, a light and all but weightless piece. In the evening light passes through the mass softly and diffusely, and the composition does not dazzle but sets a mood — an Eastern evening, shadow and gold.

## Where it works

Living rooms, lobbies, dining rooms, reception rooms, restaurants. The depth reads from the side: the composition is hung at eye level rather than up under the ceiling.

## Style

Eastern classicism and Art Deco: the arch as a decorative motif. The message is warm and atmospheric — a piece for an interior with an Eastern note.

## Made to order

The number of arches, the depth of the layers and the scale are all made to order.
