## Description

A composition about a sky mixed with a forest. Soft lumps — white and mossy green — read as fragments of a cumulus sky and as fragments of a canopy at the same time. The form is deliberately alive: no sharp corners, no geometry, as though it had grown by itself.

The white ones give purity and air, the green ones nature and calm; some versions add mirrored gold details, like flecks of sun in foliage.

By day it is a calm mass with not one straight line — a piece read through plasticity and the alternation of colour. In the evening light comes out from under the clouds as soft warm patches, lifting the neighbouring elements, and the cloud acquires depth: what burns is not the surface but the gaps.

## Where it works

Bedrooms, living rooms, spa areas, restaurants. It gathers into a dense low cloud or scatters across a ceiling in occasional groups — the format adapts to the room.

## Style

Bio-design and natural minimalism: cloud and moss as paired motifs. The message is calm and natural — a piece for an interior that soothes.

## Made to order

The size and density of the cloud and the balance of white to green are all made to order.
