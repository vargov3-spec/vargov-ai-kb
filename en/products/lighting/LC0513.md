## Description

A composition about swirled syrup. The bubbled mass in the green-and-amber "rings" reads as a viscous liquid with bubbles caught in it — movement stopped halfway.

Every element is threaded on a gold funnel axis and resembles a spinning top frozen mid-turn. The swarm hangs at differing heights, narrowing downward: the sense of rotation survives in complete stillness.

By day it is warm amber volumes with bubbles seen against the light — a piece read through form and inner drawing, dense and comfortable. In the evening light burns in the axis and floods the volume from within: the bubbles flare like sparks in swirled syrup, and the tops begin to turn.

## Where it works

Spaces with a retro-futurist character — bar counters, kitchen islands, studies, lounges. The dense swarm requires no great height.

## Style

Retro-futurism and mid-century: a sixties form without quotation. The message is playful and warm — a piece for an interior with humour.

The number of tops, their calibres and the depth of tone are all made to order.
