## Description

A composition about the spindle. A rod with a thickening at its middle is among the oldest tools there is, and its form has not changed in thousands of years because there is nothing in it to change. The author takes it as it stands and adds only what the spindle never had: light.

Some rods carry two thickenings, some one — the rhythm is uneven. The composition is vertical, the rods hanging in a dense group at several levels.

By day it is a set of clear instruments — precise, cool forms with gold collars and finials. In the evening light enters from above and below and travels along the rod to meet in the middle: the transverse scoring breaks it into close horizontal strokes, while the thickened disc gathers it and flares into a bright ring. A double graphic — fine hatching along the rod and a bright point at the swell.

## Where it works

Living rooms with high ceilings, stairwells, lobbies, meeting rooms. The vertical group builds downward: allow for height.

## Style

Industrial minimalism and craft aesthetics: the tool as form. The message is rational — a piece for an interior that values how things are made.

## Made to order

The overall size, the number of rods, the number of swells and the finish of the collars are all made to order.
