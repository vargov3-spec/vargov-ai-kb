## Description

A composition about equilibrium. A disc on a fine needle axis — a spinning top stopped in perfect balance: a position that in reality lasts fractions of a second.

The swarm hovers at differing heights; the monochrome makes it light and strict at once: the composition plays on light and form alone, without one colour.

By day it is clear parasols on needles — a piece read through silhouettes and ribbing, light and almost graphic. In the evening light burns in the axis beneath the disc, and the ribbed surface drives it round in a silver radiance: every top acquires a glowing halo, and the swarm begins to seem to be turning.

## Where it works

Contemporary spaces — above a table, in a meeting room, in an entrance hall, in a lobby. The flat discs steal no height: the composition suits medium ceilings too.

## Style

Contemporary minimalism and optical art: rotation as subject. The message is technical and playful — a piece for an interior of clean geometry.

## Made to order

The number of tops, their calibres and their heights are all made to order.
