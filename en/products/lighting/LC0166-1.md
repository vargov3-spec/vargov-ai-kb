## Description

The same scatter, assembled from modules. The composition is counted in elements: each is a finished form, and any number of them makes up a field of the required size. There is no set arrangement — there is a principle from which both an intimate group above a table and a field across the whole ceiling can grow.

A free layout is essential: denser at the centre, sparser at the edges, with a wavy lower edge. Rank and file would kill the image — seeds are not laid out in rows.

By day it is a warm scatter in the air: the elements read through silhouette and overlap, the field calm and even. In the evening the finishes begin to behave differently — gold flares sharply, clear gives white sparks, green fills with a mute tone — and the mass shimmers unevenly, like a crown in the wind.

## Where it works

Long dining tables, bar counters, meeting rooms, lobbies, retail galleries. The modularity allows the composition to be assembled for any geometry: a dense group, a drawn-out line or a continuous field.

## Style

Organic modernism with a jeweller's accent: the module instead of ornament, natural form instead of decor. The message is warm — a piece for an interior that loves gold but does not love ceremony.

## Made to order

The number of elements, the area of the field, the ratio of finishes and the height of the suspensions are all made to order.
