## Description

A composition about a galaxy of one's own. Planets hang in different orbits, elongated golden elements stitch the space between them like rockets on the launch pad, and the whole system opens slowly as you move through the room.

The round elements read as the surfaces of planets: a relief texture with craters and seas, lit around the edge by a fine ring of light.

By day it is a sculptural installation with a play of matte against mirrored: the relief reads through light and shade, the polished bodies catch the room. In the evening the room goes out and only space remains — glowing planet discs, trajectories, stars.

## Where it works

Children's rooms, dark evening interiors, lobbies, stairwells, showrooms. The effect works with subdued ambient light.

## Style

Cosmic modernism and mid-century: the orbit as a motif. The message is fairy-tale and contemporary — a piece for a space that loves stories.

## Made to order

The number of planets and rockets, the diameters and the drop heights are all made to order.
