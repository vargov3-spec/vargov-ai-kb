# Light composition LC0601

## Description

A composition about a flock at dawn. The birds are lit from below — the way they look at the hour when the sun is still under the horizon and the light comes from the ground rather than the sky. That one device sets the whole time of day inside the piece.

Every figure is cast at a moment of movement: spread wings with plumage in relief, a body that has caught a rising current. There is no prescribed scheme — the birds line up in a wedge, scatter into a free flock, rise in a spiral or draw out into a train.

By day it is a sculpture in the air: a white matte finish and warm golden metal keep the composition in a noble, calm register, and the piece reads through the plasticity of the plumage. In the evening the sources under the wings come on and the flock carries the light — a dawn that arrives on schedule.

## Where it works

Double-height living rooms, staircases, hotel lobbies, galleries. The same series becomes an intimate group above a table or an installation tens of metres long.

## Style

Art Nouveau and contemporary sculpture: flight as a classic motif. The message is poetic — a piece that stops a moment.

## Made to order

The number of birds, the trajectory of the flight and the height and direction of each are all made to order.
