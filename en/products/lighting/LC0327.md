## Description

A bridge thrown across where a bridge has nothing to come from. Two ribbons meet at the apex and part toward the edges, breaking off at a crumpled edge — an arch resting on nothing.

The green here is deep, bottle-dark, with a cold undertone — a rare colour for large light objects and memorable for that reason.

The model holds an international award. The intent is architectural gesture: the composition does not hang over a space, it spans it like a vault.

By day the ribbing breaks the deep bottle tone into dozens of shades: in one place the ribbon is almost black, in another it glows grass-green. In the evening the vault lights up from within, and the arch that rests on nothing turns out to be glowing as well.

## Where it works

A format for wide spaces: above a long table, in an entrance hall, in a restaurant room, in a lobby, in a winter garden.

## Style

Eco-modern and contemporary classic with a botanical note: live plants, natural textures, muted green in the finishes. The message is natural force — the colour of a forest brought into architectural form.

## Made to order

The span of the arch, the tone and the drop height are all made to order.
