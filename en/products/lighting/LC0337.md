## Description

Three spheres threaded vertically — and three different views of one object: from the ground floor you see the lowest sphere, from the first the middle one, from above the whole row at once.

The structure is openwork throughout: through the weave you see both the neighbouring sphere and the space beyond it. The volume here is declared but not occupied — a form consisting mostly of air.

The intent is in the vertical. The composition does not hang above the viewer, it accompanies them on the way up, opening in parts — light tied to movement.

By day the structure is openwork throughout: through the weave you see both the neighbouring sphere and the space beyond it — the volume is declared but not occupied. In the evening light is born inside the spheres and comes out through the weave as a net of rays — three glowing cocoons threaded on a vertical.

## Where it works

Ideal for a stairwell: the spheres read from different floors in turn. Also good in a narrow double-height entrance hall and in a shaft between floors.

## Style

Contemporary Art Nouveau and soft high-tech: open staircases, glass balustrades, spare finishes. The message is upward movement — three steps of light leading the eye along a vertical.

## Made to order

The number of spheres, their diameters and the density of the weave are all made to order.
