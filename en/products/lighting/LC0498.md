## Description

A composition about a formula written in the air. Hexagonal honeycomb frames are linked into a horizontal chain — a structure out of a chemistry textbook carried into space. At the nodes of the lattice, white loop-petals open: strict geometry and living organics work off each other.

That contrast is exactly what carries the piece: the lattice sets the order, the petals break it.

By day it is gold graphic work with white accents — the chain reads through the lines of the cells, an elongated and strict piece. In the evening light comes on inside the petals, the cells go into shadow, and a row of glowing points remains, threaded on an invisible formula.

## Where it works

Dining tables, bar counters, reception desks — long spaces that need holding with one continuous line. The chain is calculated for the length of the zone.

## Style

Bauhaus and natural Art Nouveau: the lattice against the plant. The message is intellectual — a piece for an interior that loves a contradiction.

## Made to order

The length of the chain, the number of cells and the density of the petals are all made to order.
