# Light composition LC0471

## Description

A composition about scales. Three-dimensional pyramids hang as a dense swarm, facet to facet — that is how scales lie: element against element, without gaps, with the planes all facing one way.

Milky facets in a gold setting: two substances divided by role — one glows, the other outlines.

By day it is a dense swarm of geometric forms: the gold frames read as graphic work, the milky facets through light and shade, a piece strict and warm at once. In the evening the milky facets glow evenly, the gold frames flare with reflections, and the swarm begins to shift like scales turning.

## Where it works

Above a table, in an entrance hall, in a lift lobby, in a meeting room. The dense swarm requires no great height — the composition is compact vertically.

## Style

Art Deco and contemporary minimalism: strict geometry in a warm register. The message is restrained and expensive — a piece for an interior that values order.

## Made to order

The number of elements, their calibres and the density of the swarm are all made to order.
