# Light composition LC0531

## Description

A composition about rain arrested in a body. Inside every faceted lozenge, gold sparks shimmer — drops caught at the moment of falling and left there for good.

Lozenges of differing size hang at differing heights: the swarm is deep and layered and reassembles as you walk around it, like a kaleidoscope.

By day it is smoky gold — a rare, adult register: the composition looks expensive and restrained at once, read through faceting and inner sparks. In the evening light burns inside every crystal and the facets hand it out to the space in sharp warm highlights — the swarm begins to throw light across the walls.

## Where it works

Living rooms, dining rooms, studies, lobbies, reception rooms. The layered swarm requires height and reads as you walk around it.

## Style

Art Deco and jewelled Art Nouveau: the smoky stone as a gem. The message is restrained and expensive — a piece for an interior with adult taste.

## Made to order

The number of crystals, their sizes and the density of the swarm are all made to order.
