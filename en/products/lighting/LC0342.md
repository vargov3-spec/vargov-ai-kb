## Description

There is no natural form here — only pure structure. The object is built like a thought: a horizontal, a vertical, a node, a pause.

The light lives inside the lines and never comes out as a patch — it rules the space, leaving air and shadow between the rods. By day the frame reads; in the evening only a glowing drawing remains, suspended with no support.

The intent is built on a contradiction: a rigid orthogonal grid filled with a texture that resists it — sparks inside the rods make the strict geometry alive.

## Where it works

Contemporary spaces with high ceilings: lofts, open-plan offices, atriums, galleries, showrooms, meeting rooms.

## Style

High-tech, constructivism, industrial minimalism: concrete, metal, exposed services. The message is intellectual: this is a structure rather than an ornament, and it declares the rationality of its owner.

## Made to order

The dimensions of the lattice, the density of the rods and the finish of the capsules are all made to order.
