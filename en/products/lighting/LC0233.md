## Description

A branch that has wound itself around a space. The base is curved into a broad loop in the horizontal plane — it reads not as a structure but as something that grew and took the place.

The foliage comes in three kinds: clear with relief, opaque white and mirrored gold. They are mixed without system, so the crown looks grown rather than assembled. A second reading also exists — wholly clear, where the entire mass becomes almost colourless.

By day it is a branch taken from a tree and gilded — dry, graphic. In the evening the foliage lights up unevenly: the clear glows right through, the matte smoulders in patches, the mirrored reflects its neighbours — one crown, three different fires.

## Where it works

An elongated horizontal format — for long tables, kitchen islands, meeting rooms, reception areas, restaurant floors.

## Style

Eco-modernism and contemporary classicism: warm timber, brass, living plants. The message is natural disorder — a composition about how nature never lines its elements up in a row.

## Made to order

The length of the branch, the composition of the foliage, the palette and the density of the crown are all made to order.
