## Description

The man-made pretending to have been found. The crystals are split irregularly, the facets meet at random angles, and the object looks lifted out of rock rather than assembled in a workshop.

Light enters at the bases and turns every facet into a prism: some crystals burn from within, others only reflect their neighbours. A depth arises between them that exists in no single element.

That is the device: the composition is built from repeating modules and reads as a one-of-a-kind mineral. Order pretends to be chance — deliberately and all the way through.

By day it is rock: the split facets catch daylight unevenly and the cluster looks lifted out of the ground. In the evening light enters at the bases and every facet becomes a prism — some crystals burn from within, others only reflect their neighbours, and a depth opens inside the mass.

## Where it works

The vertical cluster asks for height: a staircase, an atrium, a double-height entrance hall, a study with double light.

## Style

Contemporary luxury and brutal opulence: rough stone, dark metal, leather, coarse texture. The message is the primordial — the piece speaks of the force of nature in the language of precise work.

## Made to order

The number of crystals, their calibres and the finish of the branches are all made to order.
