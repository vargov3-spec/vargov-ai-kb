## Description

A composition about frozen rain. The tubes descend in a dense cascade — jets caught by frost halfway down. The lower edge breathes: long and short alternate like the edge of melting ice.

Inside, a bubbled mass; outside, chrome tips: a cold, clean note held all the way through.

By day it is clear verticals with bubbles seen against the light — a piece read through rhythm and the ragged lower edge, strict and bright. In the evening thousands of bubbles flare along the whole length, and every tube turns into a column of radiance: the ice catches fire from within.

## Where it works

Stairwells and the zone above a kitchen island, where verticals read at full height; contemporary spaces with panoramic windows and spare finishes. The cascade is designed for the height.

## Style

Contemporary minimalism and high-tech: cold as an aesthetic position. The message is clean and technical — a piece for an interior with no embellishment.

## Made to order

The number of tubes, their lengths and the format of the cascade are all made to order.
