## Description

A composition about a garland that stayed for good. The branch spreads above the table as one continuous line — a festive gesture stripped of its occasion and therefore fit for every day.

The leaves have a lifelike curve, as though they had just unfurled; between them, points of light burn in the branch.

By day it is white on polished gold — the clean graphic of a leaf, a dressy and light piece. In the evening the points lift the gloss from below and the leaves begin to throw warm highlights onto one another: the garland comes alive the way a real branch comes alive under a lamp.

## Where it works

Dining rooms, living rooms, lobbies, restaurants, reception desks. The horizontal line is calculated for the length of the table or the counter.

## Style

Art Nouveau and contemporary classic: white-and-gold botany as the signature formula. The message is festive and hospitable — a piece for the table people are received at.

## Made to order

The length of the branch, the density of the foliage and the number of lights are all made to order.
