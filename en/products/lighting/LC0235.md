## Description

A lens instead of a lamp. The elements do not glow of themselves — they gather light arriving from above and from the side, and work as optics rather than as a source.

The curvature of every crescent is arranged so that a gold core appears in its depth while a glowing thread runs along the thickened rim. The form is transparent to the point of invisibility, yet the light within it is visible — the object disappears and the effect remains.

By day the composition barely reads: only highlights and contours against a pale ground. In the evening it becomes a cloud of warm lights, each set into an invisible mount.

## Where it works

An elongated horizontal format — for long dining tables, bar counters, meeting rooms, generous halls, restaurants.

## Style

Contemporary minimalism and optical design: white walls, clean lines, an absence of ornament. The message is light without a luminaire — the composition gives all attention to the effect and removes the object.

## Made to order

The length, the number of elements, the density of the layers and the colour temperature are all made to order.
