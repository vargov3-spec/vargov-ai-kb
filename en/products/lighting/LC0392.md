## Description

A composition about an orchestra before a concert. Trumpets of differing calibre look up, down and sideways — that is how a brass section looks while the players tune up: the directions are random, but everyone is doing one job.

The branches wind into a dense tangle, the green elements are used sparingly — they enliven the white mass and add a botanical note without turning the piece into a plant.

By day it is white with olive on chrome: a matte surface, polished metal, a composition read through the form of the trumpets. In the evening light comes out of the depth of each one as a soft cone — the composition delivers genuine working light rather than only an image, and the different directions wash the room from several sides.

## Where it works

Living rooms, dining rooms, lobbies, meeting rooms. The trumpets are aimed at assembly: the composition can light the table, the wall or the ceiling as chosen.

## Style

Art Nouveau and modernism: botany and mechanics in one form. The message is practical and alive — a piece for the image and for the light alike.

## Made to order

The number of trumpets, the share of green and the dimensions of the tangle are all made to order.
