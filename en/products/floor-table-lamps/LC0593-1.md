## Description

The same bouquet, opened wider. The umbrellas are spread across the height and turned in different directions — each catches its own angle, and the vertical stops being a stand with lights on top: it becomes a plant reaching several ways at once.

The spread of tilts gives the piece movement: from one point it is a dense crown, from another a sparse branch.

By day it is fine botany at full height: the pleating reads as hatching, the golden stems draw the vertical, the piece is light and tall. In the evening each disc lights up separately and gains a halo of its own — the lamp gives not one circle of light but several small ones, at different heights. A garden standing beside the armchair.

## Where it works

The corner of a sitting room, the zone by a window, a bedroom, a reception room. Umbrellas facing different ways let the light be tuned to the task: some down for reading, some up for atmosphere.

## Style

Art Nouveau and organic modernism: growth as the principle of form. The message is warm and alive — a piece for an interior that loves nature.

## Made to order

The height of the stand, the number of umbrellas and the spread of heights and tilts are all made to order.
