## Description

A composition about structure that does not hide. Chromed rods run right through the column, and the author does not mask them — on the contrary, broad gaps are left between the gold blades so the frame stays visible. The piece honestly shows how it is made.

The blades — a broad leaf with a sharp tip and a slight twist — run in tiers from bottom to top, overlapping. Each is turned on its axis its own way: the column comes out transparent despite the density of metal.

By day it is a sculpture in two metals: cool chrome inside, warm gold outside, and the contrast holds the object. In the evening the light comes from below, strikes the underside of every blade and reflects upward — the belly lights up while the top stays dark. Deep inside the column the chrome catches the gold reflections and multiplies them.

## Where it works

Living rooms, halls, reception rooms, bars, showrooms. Light reflected upward lifts the ceiling — the lamp works as a source of volume, not only as an accent.

## Style

Industrial luxury and Art Deco: exposed structure, expensive finish. The message is technical — a piece for someone who cares how a thing is made.

## Made to order

The column height, the number of blades, the metal finish and the height of the light unit are all made to order.
