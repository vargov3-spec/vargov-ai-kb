## Description

A composition about a shoot that has broken through a column. The stems set a strict vertical rhythm, and a clear twisted form appears inside it — like movement arrested where it has no business being.

The proportions are calibrated on growth: the drawn-out vertical emphasises the upward movement, and placing the forms at different levels creates an inner rhythm.

By day it is a bundle of gold stems with clear sails at the top: the bubbles in the mass read against the light, a strict and light piece. In the evening light burns at the heart of every sail, the bubbles flare as a constellation, and the clear mass throws live highlights onto the tubes — the strict column begins to breathe.

## Where it works

The corners of a living room, the zone beside a window, wall piers, reception rooms. The vertical takes little floor area and works both as a source and as a sculpture.

## Style

Organic Art Nouveau and architectural minimalism: growth within order. The message is alive — a piece for an interior that loves contrast.

## Made to order

The height, the finish of the stems and the number of clear elements are all made to order.
