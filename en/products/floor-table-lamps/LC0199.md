## Description

A weave that reads differently from every side. A white ribbon runs inward, a gold rod comes out, the next ribbon covers it again — volume is built by alternation rather than by thickness.

The lower half is left empty: bare rods only. All the mass and all the light gather at the top, at the eye level of a standing person, while the lower part works as a leg.

By day it is a sculptural column with rich plasticity — you can see how one and the same ribbon is white at one edge and shows a gold lining at the other. In the evening the light emerges through the cells of the lattice, and the column begins to glow from within, evenly, without a single direct point.

## Where it works

Living rooms, halls, reception rooms, lounges, showrooms, hotel lobbies.

## Style

Contemporary Art Deco and Eastern luxury: brass, deep tones, rugs, carving. The message is craft — a composition about the maker's hand, legible in every interlacing.

## Made to order

The column height, the density of the weave, the finish of the rods and the colour of the ribbons are all made to order.
