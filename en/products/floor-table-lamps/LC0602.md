# Floor lamp LC0602

## Description

The same flock in a floor format. Around a vertical pole the birds circle in a rising spiral, and the eye reads the movement from the bottom up: the flock takes off and the light rises with it.

The light here is not a function but company: it stands beside you like something alive.

By day the floor lamp works as a sculpture: the fine plasticity of the plumage, a clean golden vertical, the rhythm of the wings. In the evening it works as a source of soft, living light that does not flood but is scattered along the silhouette, like windows at dusk.

## Where it works

A living room, a bedroom, a study, a reception room. It is an independent object and at the same time a continuation of the LC0601 series: a suspended flock beneath the ceiling and a rising flock beside an armchair gather a space into one story.

## Style

Art Nouveau and contemporary sculpture: the vertical as a trajectory of take-off. The message is lyrical — a piece for an interior that loves stories.

## Made to order

The number of birds, their placement on the pole and the finish are all made to order.
