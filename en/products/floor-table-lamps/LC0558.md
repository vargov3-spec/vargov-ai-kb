## Description

A composition about water arrested mid-fall. The contour holds a strict geometry — the tubes rise in a bundle and finish in smooth arches; the drops inside break it softly and naturally.

Order and its breaking work as a pair: without the arches the drops would be random, without the drops the arches would be empty.

By day it is gold arches with clear twisted drops: the bubbles read against the light, a rhythmic and composed piece. In the evening light passes through the drop and fills it with an inner radiance, while highlights run down the tubes toward the base — the light quite literally flows down the structure.

## Where it works

Living rooms, bedrooms, reception rooms, studies. It takes little space and gathers a whole room around it. Related to the LC0567 wall light — together they give one language of light for walls and floor.

## Style

Art Deco and contemporary classic: the arch and the drop as a classic pairing. The message is elegant — a piece for an interior with poise.

## Made to order

The height, the number of drops and the finish of the tubes are all made to order.
