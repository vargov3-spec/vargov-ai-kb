## Description

The same wave stood upright. Clear blocks are assembled into a strict column inside which a fluid form rolls: strict outside, alive within.

The light does not come out directly — it slides along the bends, refracts and fills the mass with depth.

By day it is a clear column with a bubbled texture: a piece read through distortions and inner drawing, almost without mass. In the evening the light reveals the depth — the mass fills and the wave inside becomes visible entire. A black frame with turquoise light gives a nocturnal, electric reading; a gold one with warm light an intimate, jewel-like one.

## Where it works

The corner of a living room, a study, a bedroom, a reception room. The vertical takes little floor area and works both as a source and as a sculpture.

## Style

Contemporary minimalism and optical art: refraction as the content. The message is technical and mesmerising — a piece for an interior with contemporary taste.

## Made to order

The height, the finish of the frame and the colour of the light are all made to order.
