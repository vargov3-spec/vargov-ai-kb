## Description

A composition about the lining. Every element is a broad sickle with a deep hollow: white and opaque outside, polished gold within. The author turns each element on its axis its own way, and the column begins to play at openness: one shows a white back, the next reveals a gold interior.

The tiers run unevenly, with gatherings and gaps — you read a branch with large leaves, or an inflorescence opening upward. The stem and base are in the same gold: the structure is reduced to a single vertical, all attention on the crown.

By day it is graphics — white silhouettes on a gold shaft, sculpture without light. In the evening the gold within heats to a honeyed tone and throws reflected light outward: not one direct point, only a warm radiance out of the hollows. The white outside stays cool. The piece literally glows from inside.

## Where it works

A large crown and soft reflected light — living rooms, halls, reception rooms, showrooms, restaurant areas. It needs height to spare and a clear wall behind it.

## Style

Hollywood glamour and contemporary Art Deco: white with gold, the contrast of opaque and mirrored. The message is representative — a piece for an interior that receives guests.

## Made to order

The height, the number of elements, the gilding finish and the colour of the outer surface are all made to order.
