## Description

A composition about a bouquet in which the light has opened. From the base rises a bundle of golden stems, and on each a pleated parasol unfurls — a form that reads as a giant water-lily pad, as a flower head and as a sea fan.

The parasols stand at differing heights: a bouquet, not a formation.

By day it is fine botany on gold stems: the corrugation reads through light and shade, and the deep green velvet of the base gives the piece weight and warmth. In the evening light comes on in the gold core of every parasol, rays radiate along the folds, and each disc acquires a halo of its own — a soft shadow below, a glowing relief above. A small garden of light beside an armchair.

## Where it works

Beside an armchair, beside a bed, in a private living room, in a boudoir. Several blooms at differing heights replace both a floor lamp and a night light — the light is adjusted by tilting the parasols.

## Style

Art Nouveau and contemporary classic: botany with velvet and gilding. The message is intimate and warm — a piece for a private space.

## Made to order

The number and tilt of the parasols, the finish of the stems and the colour of the base are all made to order.
