## Description

The same crown on a light base — and the object changes character. You can move it with one hand; it does not bind a zone to itself the way the stone versions do.

The light emerges reflected off the gold faces: no direct source is visible from any position. For a lamp standing at the eye level of a seated person, that matters.

By day it is a decorative sculpture on a stem — mobile, almost weightless. In the evening it gives a soft warm patch around a chair: not illumination for a room, but a circle in which people sit.

## Where it works

Living rooms, bedrooms, studies, zones beside a chair or sofa, hotel rooms, reading corners.

## Style

Contemporary classicism and comfortable minimalism: pale walls, textiles, timber. The message is personal space — a composition about light for one person.

## Made to order

The height, the crown diameter, the number of ribbons and the base finish are all made to order.
