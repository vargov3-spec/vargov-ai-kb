## Description

A composition about an object of the inner circle. The piece lives on a table, a console or a shelf, at arm's length — where the texture is visible and the character can be heard. This is not an accent for a room but a companion.

The folds radiate from the centre in uneven rays — the skirt of a jellyfish or a gramophone horn compressed at the moment of sound. In the smoky version the surface is deep and mirrored; in the version with gold inclusions, thousands of warm particles shimmer in the mass.

By day the object changes constantly with the angle of view and with whatever is happening around it: every facet catches a highlight of its own. In the evening light leaves the core and spreads through the folds from within — the relief appears entire and the object becomes a glowing flower.

## Where it works

A desk, a console in a hallway, a shelf in a living room, a bedside table. The compact format requires no ceiling space and no wiring.

## Style

Art Deco and marine modern: the horn as a form. The message is intimate — a piece for a personal space.

The execution, the finish of the core and the calibre are all made to order.
