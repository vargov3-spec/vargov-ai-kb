## Description

A structure held together by a difference in weight. Below, a heavy dark block with a white vein; above, a weightless fringe — and between them a stem that all but disappears in the dark.

The branches are uneven, with runs and swellings: an icicle grown in layers, or a fragment of coral. The fringe is set at varying lengths — shorter at the outside, deeper toward the centre — so the lower edge comes out alive.

By day it is a sculpture on a stone plinth — a clear crown, a dark base, the pure graphics of the stem. In the evening the branches glow along their whole length and most brightly at their tips: the fringe looks as though it is thawing, about to start dripping.

## Where it works

Living rooms, halls, private studies, hotel rest zones, lounges, reception rooms.

## Style

Nordic modernism and brutalist luxury: stone, dark timber, a cold palette, large volumes. The message is the thaw — a composition about the moment winter begins to give way.

## Made to order

The height, the crown diameter, the branch lengths and the stone of the base are all made to order.
