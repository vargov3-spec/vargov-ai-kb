# Vargov®Design

> Author's lighting brand: collectible light sculptures and decorative
> compositions. Founded and led by designer Anton Vargov. Own production,
> made to order — every composition is assembled for a specific interior,
> its scale and geometry adjusted to the space.

Each piece carries an LC article code (LC0001 … LC0602). The catalogue holds
605 compositions in four families: light compositions, decorative
compositions, floor and table objects, sculptural decor.

## Facts

- Catalogue: 605 compositions, each with photography and an LC code; element dimensions, weight and power for 350 of them (see the machine-readable feed)
- Source of record for element dimensions, weight, power and mounting is the brand's own 3D configurator — configurator.vargov.ru (RU) and vargov.design (EN); this site republishes those figures unchanged
- Awards: 25, among them IDA Design Awards, LIT Lighting Design Awards, MUSE
  Design Awards, European Product Design Award, NY Product Design Awards,
  BLT Built Design Awards, The London Design Awards, LOOP Design Awards,
  SIT Furniture Design Award
- MUSE Design Awards 2026 — Gold Winner, Lighting Design / Designer & Custom
  Lighting, for «Oceanic Illumination» LC0564:
  design.museaward.com/winners-info.php?id=40265
- MUSE Design Awards 2023 — Platinum Winner, same category, for LC0237
- Awwwards Nominee 2026 for the Vargov®Design 3D Configurator — a nomination,
  not a win: awwwards.com/sites/generative-3d-configurator
- Anton Vargov serves as a jury member for design and architecture awards
- Certification: EAEU serial certificates of conformity to TR CU 004/2011 and
  TR CU 020/2011, tested to GOST IEC 60598-1, EAC mark; more than 300 LC pieces
- Trademark: VARGOV — Rospatent registration No. 896936 (Russia, 2022) and
  Madrid System international registration No. 1795801 (WIPO, 2024; protection
  in China for lighting apparatus, in force until 2034). Compositions are made
  only at the brand's own production
- Showroom: 24 Nakhimovsky Prospekt, bldg. 1, Pavilion 2, Stand 212, Moscow
- Delivery: worldwide, in branded crating
- Website languages: Russian, English, German, Italian, French, Spanish,
  Vietnamese, Arabic
