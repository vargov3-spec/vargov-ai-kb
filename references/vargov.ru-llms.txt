# Vargov®Design

> Author's lighting brand: collectible light sculptures and decorative
> compositions. Founded and led by designer Anton Vargov. The brand's first
> designs date from 2018. Own production, made to order — every composition
> is assembled for a specific interior, its scale and geometry adjusted to
> the space.

Each piece carries an LC article code (LC0001 … LC0602). The catalogue holds
605 compositions in 4 families: Lighting compositions; Decorative compositions; Floor lamps, sconces, table art objects; Sculptural compositions & décor.

## For architects and specifiers

- [For architects](https://vargov.ru/en/architects): how bespoke projects run, from sketch to installation
- [How many ceiling mounting points a light fixture needs](https://vargov.ru/en/guides/mounting-points): one point per element for a composition on cables, one node for a single-body fitting, and the nine SKUs where neither rule holds
- [How much a large glass composition weighs](https://vargov.ru/en/guides/chandelier-weight): eight calculated compositions, 4.3 to 447.7 kg, with element counts, mounting points and load per point; one composition laid out seven ways, 1.8 to 6.8 kg
- [Online lighting configurator with IFC4 (BIM) export for architects](https://vargov.ru/en/configurator/ifc4): what the IFC4 export holds and how to open it in Revit, ArchiCAD and other BIM software — the class set per element: lit elements as IfcLightFixture, unlit ones as IfcFurnishingElement; every ceiling fixing point a separate IfcDiscreteAccessory with its own load (property set Vargov_Mount: SuspensionType, ElementsAtPoint, LoadKg); the file drops into the model with no manual offset — built for the ceiling height set in the Ceiling height field next to the IFC button (2700 mm by default; property CeilingHeight in Vargov_Design on IfcBuilding), fixing points on the ceiling, origin on the floor; a PDF specification and a tender sheet alongside; 360 of 605 compositions open with a full calculation; no Revit family (.rfa); DXF drawings are for internal use only: the brand's production and dealers
- [FBX model from the configurator](https://vargov.ru/en/configurator/fbx): a composition assembled in the configurator downloads as FBX and opens in 3ds Max — separate objects with colours, threads, ceiling fixings and a ceiling plane, at real size, no rescaling
- [Production](https://vargov.ru/en/production): from sketch and mock-up to hand assembly, 1:1 installation drawings and installation on site; individual production and the ready elements in stock
- [Installation](https://vargov.ru/en/installation): suspension, load, wiring, service access
- [Installation guide (PDF)](https://vargov.ru/pdf/vargov-installation-en.pdf): mounting step by step, from marking out the fixing points to fitting the elements
- [Connection guide (PDF)](https://vargov.ru/pdf/vargov-connection-en.pdf): electrical connection: parallel wiring only, splitting into branches, sizing the driver, first switch-on
- Both guides exist in all 8 site languages: /pdf/vargov-installation-<lang>.pdf and /pdf/vargov-connection-<lang>.pdf, where <lang> is ru, en, de, it, fr, es, vi or ar
- [Certification](https://vargov.ru/en/certification): documents, standards, EAC
- [Warranty](https://vargov.ru/en/warranty): what is covered, how originals are identified
- [Configurator](https://vargov.ru/en/configurator): assemble a composition, export an IFC4 (BIM) model, a PDF specification and a tender sheet, and get a quote
- [Element colour: RAL painting and electroplating](https://vargov.ru/en/specifications/colours): glass, crystal and ceramic elements painted in any of the 210 RAL Classic colours (glass and crystal translucent from 10 to 90 % or opaque, ceramics opaque only) or electroplated — transparent tints for glass and crystal, mirror gold, rose gold and chrome for all three; the brief names the RAL code or the finish
- [Metal finishes on video](https://vargov.ru/en/specifications/finishes): the JN-9001…JN-9024 finishes of the metal fittings — eight colours, each mirror-polished, brushed with gloss lacquer or brushed with semi-matte lacquer; the physical finish catalogue can be seen at the showroom
